\title{WISE: Self-Sustaining Population Equilibria\\
over Decision-Induced Robot Networks}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Jorge Luis Mayorga Taborda}
\IEEEauthorblockA{\textit{Universidad Internacional de Valencia (VIU)}\\
Valencia, Spain\\
jl.mayorga236@gmail.com}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Distributed population-game dynamics need a connected communication graph to
estimate the aggregates and payoffs that define their equilibria. We study the
self-referential case in which the equilibrium's own spatial composition
determines whether that graph exists. For a heterogeneous multi-robot team we
show that the productively optimal aggregate is unique but its \emph{composition}
is degenerate---a positive-dimensional fiber of equal-payoff profiles---so
classical theory fixes how much wrench each load receives, not which robot types
provide it. On that fiber we place an endogenous, decision-induced candidate-site
Laplacian $\Lap(x)$ and define the \emph{Wrench- and Information-Self-Sustaining
Equilibrium} (WISE): a
productively optimal composition that also keeps algebraic connectivity above the
stability threshold $\sigmadyn=\vt^2/(c\,\mF)$ (WISE requires the margin-inflated
$\sigmastar=\sigmadyn+\delta$). We prove (i) composition degeneracy with a
productive-nullspace dimension formula, (ii) a \emph{free/costly/impossible}
trichotomy for information self-sustainability governed by the connectivity maxima
$\LamE$ over the optimal fiber and $\LamX$ over the feasible set, (iii) a robust
local self-computation certificate $c\,\mF\,\lamtwo>\vt^2$ derived from the coupled
strategy--estimator dynamics (worst-case tight in the canonical aligned block), and
(iv) a safe selector whose productive loss vanishes as the information reward
$\varepsilon\downarrow0$. Closed-loop simulation with rigid-load transport and
second-order unicycles---with verified integer recovery---supports the theory;
relay roles emerge by comparative advantage. \emph{A population equilibrium can produce a configuration that
destroys the graph its own algorithm requires; composition heterogeneity is
exactly what lets it select the equilibria that survive.}
\end{abstract}
