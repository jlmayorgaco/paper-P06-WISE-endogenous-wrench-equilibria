\title{WISE: Zero-Cost Connectivity within Wrench-Feasible Assignment\\
Fibers for Heterogeneous Robot Transport}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Jorge Luis Mayorga Taborda}
\IEEEauthorblockA{\textit{Universidad Internacional de Valencia (VIU)}\\
Valencia, Spain\\
jl.mayorga236@gmail.com}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Heterogeneous multi-robot transport couples physical task allocation with the communication
structure needed to coordinate it. We show that when productive utility depends on a
low-dimensional served aggregate, its unique optimal \emph{served aggregate} can correspond to
a \emph{positive-dimensional fiber} of wrench-feasible robot compositions---a resource for
communication design. We characterize the fiber on its active face and derive a
free-networkability modulus $\Gamma_{\Emanifold}$ whose positivity is necessary \emph{and
sufficient} for a strictly better-connected composition to exist anywhere on the fiber, so
that $\Gamma_{\Emanifold}=0$ certifies global connectivity optimality, repeated Fiedler
eigenvalues included. A wrench- and information-self-sustaining equilibrium (WISE) is selected
lexicographically by a semidefinite program, and the resulting spectral capacities separate
\emph{zero-cost}, \emph{costly} and \emph{impossible} requirements through a price of
self-sustainability whose subgradient prices connectivity in units of utility. Clearing the
derived threshold buys exponential stability of a reduced information layer, and the same
certified lower rate bound holds under a moving physical graph. A planar closed-loop transport
experiment makes the distinction physical: relative to the productively identical but
disconnected baseline, the WISE composition maintains the certified communication bound and
resynchronizes both disturbed loads in $30/30$ paired worlds versus $0/30$, at the same served
aggregate, productive value and nominal wrench-feasibility certificates. An exact oracle measures the integer
self-sustaining gap for $N\le8$, and structured rounding attains re-certified integer
assignments in $895/900$ draws at $N=12$. Zero-cost is exact on the relaxed fiber; finite-team
integerization may incur a separately quantified recovery loss.
\end{abstract}
