\section{Self-Sustaining Equilibria}
\label{sec:wise}

\subsection{Productive value and the optimal composition fiber}
The active load set is fixed: wrench feasibility ($z\in X_{\mathrm f}$) guarantees each
load its physical service, and the productive value allocates \emph{total served
capacity} among the admitted loads through the \emph{served-capacity} aggregate $y=Bz$,
$y_k=\sum_{\tau,h}c_\tau z_{\tau kh}$ (distinct from the wrench aggregate $H_wz$):
\begin{equation}
V(z)=\phi(Bz),\qquad
\phi(y)=\sum_k\big(v_k\,y_k-\tfrac12\alpha\,y_k^2\big),
\label{eq:value}
\end{equation}
so $\phi(y)=-\tfrac12\|y-y_{\mathrm{ref}}\|_H^2+\mathrm{const}$ with $H=\alpha I\succ0$
and $y_{\mathrm{ref}}=v/\alpha$. It is strictly concave ($\nabla^2\phi=-\alpha I\prec0$)
and so pins down a unique optimal aggregate, whereas a deficit penalty such as
$-\|[d-s]_+\|^2$ would be flat on the feasible set and leave it undetermined. The game is
a constrained potential population game with payoff $\partial V/\partial z_{\tau a}$, and
identical-interest: the novelty lies in what $V$ leaves undetermined.

Wrench feasibility is a \emph{hard} shared constraint, its projected matrix $H_w$
(the wrench facets of Lemma~\ref{prop:membership}, $H_w z\ge d$) kept distinct from the
objective Hessian $H$:
\begin{equation}
X_{\mathrm f}=\{z\in\mathcal X:\;H_w z\ge d\}.
\label{eq:Xf}
\end{equation}
The action cost $g$ enters only as a selection refinement, \emph{excluded} from the
degeneracy result.

\begin{definition}[Productive variational equilibrium]\label{def:ve}
With $F(z)=-\nabla V(z)=-B^{\top}\nabla\phi(Bz)$, a profile $z^{\star}\in X_{\mathrm f}$ is a
\emph{productive variational equilibrium} if $\langle F(z^{\star}),z-z^{\star}\rangle\ge0$
for all $z\in X_{\mathrm f}$. As $V=\phi(Bz)$ is concave on the convex $X_{\mathrm f}$, this
holds iff $z^{\star}\in\arg\max_{z\in X_{\mathrm f}}V(z)$: the productive optimum \emph{is}
the variational equilibrium~\cite{facchinei2003fvi}, and its degeneracy is
Proposition~\ref{prop:fibermono}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Composition degeneracy]\label{thm:degeneracy}
Since $\phi$ is strictly concave on the compact convex $BX_{\mathrm f}$
($H=\alpha I\succ0$), the productively optimal aggregate $y^{\star}=Bz^{\star}$ is unique;
but the optimal set $\Emanifold=\{z\in X_{\mathrm f}:Bz=y^{\star}\}$ is a nonempty compact
convex polytope whose local dimension at a relative-interior point $\bar z$ is
\begin{equation}
\dim\Emanifold=\dim\!\big(\ker A\cap\ker B\cap\ker G_I\big)
=n-\operatorname{rank}[A;B;G_I],
\label{eq:dim}
\end{equation}
where $Az=b$ is budget conservation, $Bz=y^{\star}$ the served aggregate, $G_Iz=h_I$
the inequalities of $X_{\mathrm f}$ active at $\bar z$ (nonnegativity, occupancy, active
wrench), and $n=\dim z$. Thus $\Emanifold$ is positive-dimensional iff the
\emph{productive neutral space} $N_p(\bar z)=\ker A\cap\ker B\cap\ker G_I$ is nontrivial.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Uniqueness of $y^{\star}$ is strict concavity of $\phi$. At
$\bar z\in\operatorname{relint}\Emanifold$ the minimal face $F$ fixes exactly the
active constraints, so the feasible perturbations are
$\{d:Ad=0,\,Bd=0,\,G_Id=0\}=N_p(\bar z)$; hence $\Emanifold$ locally coincides with
$\bar z+N_p(\bar z)$ and \eqref{eq:dim} is rank--nullity. Since $V=\phi(Bz)$ depends on
$z$ only through $Bz$, every $z\in\Emanifold$ attains the same $V$; a general weighted
$B$ can give $N_p=\{0\}$ (a single composition).
\end{proof}
For the representative instance ($n=72$), $\operatorname{rank}[A;B]=13$ and no inequality
is active at the interior optimum ($G_I=\varnothing$), so the fiber is full-dimensional in
its aggregate-fixing affine space: $\dim\Emanifold=72-13=59$, certified by a
relative-interior point with neutral-direction residuals
$\|Ad\|,\|Bd\|,\|G_Id\|<10^{-15}$ (dimensions computed in $z$ with the projected wrench
facets $H_w$; \texttt{exp\_fiber}). At a boundary optimum where wrench facets bind, the
same formula gives a smaller fiber.

\begin{proposition}[Fiber--monotonicity equivalence]\label{prop:fibermono}
On the active-face tangent $\mathcal T_F=\ker A\cap\ker G_I$ the pseudo-gradient
$F(z)=B^{\top}H(Bz-y_{\mathrm{ref}})$ obeys $\langle F(z{+}d)-F(z),d\rangle=\|H^{1/2}Bd\|^2$,
so its strong-monotonicity modulus is $\mu_F=\sigma_{\min}^2(H^{1/2}B|_{\mathcal T_F})$ with
$\ker(B|_{\mathcal T_F})=N_p(\bar z)$. Hence $\dim\Emanifold>0\iff\mu_F=0$: strong
monotonicity holds in the served aggregate ($H\succ0$) but is \emph{lost} on the
composition face---the degeneracy WISE exploits, unlike strongly contractive aggregative
games~\cite{martinezpiazuelo2022}.
\end{proposition}

A comparative-advantage exchange makes this concrete (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}b,c): with two
types of equal lifting capacity $c_\ell=c_s$, swapping which type lifts and which relays is
productively neutral ($Bd=0$, $V\equiv V^{\star}$) with spectral effect
$D\lamtwo[d]=\sum_{\tau,r}\gamma_{\tau r}d_{\tau r}\,v^{\top}L_r v$. Whether such a
\emph{free} gain exists is a projection condition:

\begin{proposition}[Free-connectivity criterion]\label{prop:freeconn}
Let $\bar z\in\operatorname{relint}\Emanifold$ with $\lamtwo$ simple and Fiedler vector $v$,
so the feasible directions are exactly $N_p=\ker A\cap\ker B\cap\ker G_I$ (no inequality
binds). With $g_\lambda=\nabla_z\lamtwo(\Lap(\bar z))$ (entries $\gamma_{\tau r}v^{\top}L_r
v$),
\begin{equation}
\max_{h\in N_p,\ \|h\|\le1}D\lamtwo(\bar z)[h]=\|\Pi_{N_p}g_\lambda\|,
\label{eq:freeconn}
\end{equation}
so a productively neutral connectivity improvement exists $\iff\Pi_{N_p}g_\lambda\neq0$, with
optimal direction $h^\star=\Pi_{N_p}g_\lambda/\|\Pi_{N_p}g_\lambda\|$; if it vanishes,
$\bar z$ is KKT-stationary for $\max_{\Emanifold}\lamtwo$ ($g_\lambda$ lies in the normal
cone). At a boundary $\bar z$ the same holds with $N_p$ replaced by the tangent cone
$T_{\Emanifold}(\bar z)$; and under multiplicity, with $D\lamtwo[h]$ replaced by
$\lambda_{\min}(U_2^{\top}D\Lap(\bar z)[h]\,U_2)$ ($U_2$ a basis of the reduced eigenspace),
so a first-order neutral improvement exists $\iff\max_{h\in T_{\Emanifold},\|h\|\le1}
\lambda_{\min}(U_2^{\top}D\Lap[h]U_2)>0$ (a small SDP).
\end{proposition}

\subsection{The WISE refinement}
\begin{definition}[WISE]\label{def:wise}
A profile $z^{\star}\in\Emanifold$ is a \emph{Wrench- and
Information-Self-Sustaining Equilibrium} if $\lamtwo(\Lap(z^{\star}))\ge\sigmastar$,
i.e.\ $z^{\star}\in\Ess:=\Emanifold\cap\Cdelta$, where
$\Cdelta=\{z\in X_{\mathrm f}:\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\ge\sigmastar\}$. Since
$\sigmastar=\sigmadyn+\delta>\sigmadyn$, a WISE clears the stability threshold of
Proposition~\ref{thm:stability} with margin $\delta$ and, by Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge},
keeps the \emph{geometric} graph above $\sigmadyn$. $\Cdelta$ is convex and closed
(super-level set of the concave $\lamtwo\!\circ\!\Lap$, Lemma~\ref{lem:convex}). WISE is a
\emph{lexicographic selection} from the productive VE set, not an equilibrium generated by
the productive game alone.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Relaxed WISE existence and selection as an SDP]\label{thm:existence}
Over the fluid relaxation, \emph{Stage 1} fixes the unique optimal aggregate
$y^{\star}=Bz^{\star}$, $z^{\star}\in\arg\max_{z\in X_{\mathrm f}}V(z)$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}); \emph{Stage 2} selects connectivity on the resulting
fiber. The lexicographic selector $z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}\in
\arg\max_{z\in\Emanifold}\lamtwo(\Lap(z))$ is the solution of the Stage-2 semidefinite
program, stated in the \emph{lifted} wrench variables of Lemma~\ref{prop:membership}
(so no facet enumeration of $X_{\mathrm f}$ is needed),
\begin{equation}
\LamE=\max_{z,\,\xi,\,t}\ t\quad\text{s.t.}\quad
z\in\mathcal X,\ Bz=y^{\star},\ \eqref{eq:tensor},\ Q^{\top}\Lap(z)\,Q\succeq tI,
\label{eq:sdp}
\end{equation}
convex by Lemma~\ref{lem:convex} (affine constraints in $(z,\xi)$, concave
$\lamtwo\!\circ\!\Lap$). Over the relaxation a WISE exists iff the value clears the
requirement,
\begin{equation}
\Ess\neq\varnothing\iff\LamE\ge\sigmastar,
\label{eq:exist}
\end{equation}
and any maximiser $z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}$ preserves the productive optimum exactly,
$Bz^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}=y^{\star}$, hence is itself a variational equilibrium
(Def.~\ref{def:ve}) with $V=V^{\star}$. When
$\min_{\Emanifold}\lamtwo<\sigmastar\le\LamE$ the refinement is \emph{strict}. The
argmax set $\Emanifold^{\star}$ may be non-singleton; the strongly convex tie-break
$\min_{z\in\Emanifold^{\star}}\tfrac12\|z-z_{\mathrm{prev}}\|_W^2$ ($W\succ0$, least role
change from the previous assignment) fixes a \emph{unique} selector, which a linear
$g^{\top}z$ need not.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
$X_{\mathrm f}$ is a convex polytope and $Bz=y^{\star}$ is affine; by Lemma~\ref{lem:convex},
$\{(z,t):Q^{\top}\Lap(z)Q\succeq tI\}$ is convex, so \eqref{eq:sdp} is an SDP; its value
$\LamE=\max_{z\in\Emanifold}\lamtwo(\Lap(z))$ is attained on the compact $\Emanifold$
(Weierstrass). $\Ess=\Emanifold\cap\Cdelta$ is nonempty iff this maximum reaches
$\sigmastar$, giving \eqref{eq:exist}; feasibility forces
$Bz^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}=y^{\star}$. Strictness holds when $\sigmastar$ lies
strictly inside the range of $\lamtwo$ over $\Emanifold$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Aggregate--estimator stability}
To show the \emph{dynamic significance} of the threshold (not to linearise~\eqref{eq:pd}),
posit a local optimizer--estimator model coupling a served-aggregate error $\xi\in\R^p$
and a consensus-estimator error $\eta\in\R^{(N-1)p}$,
\begin{equation}
\dot\xi=-M\xi+\Theta_1\eta,\qquad
\dot\eta=\Theta_2\,\xi-c\,(\bar L_\perp\!\otimes\! I_p)\,\eta,
\label{eq:jac}
\end{equation}
$\bar L_\perp=Q^{\top}\Lap(z^{\star})Q$, with $\tfrac12(M+M^{\top})\succeq\mF I$,
$\|\Theta_1\|_2\le\vt_1$, $\|\Theta_2\|_2\le\vt_2$, and $\mF,\vt_1,\vt_2>0$.

\begin{proposition}[Information-layer stability interpretation]\label{thm:stability}
Under those hypotheses, \eqref{eq:jac} is exponentially stable whenever
\begin{equation}
c\,\mF\,\lamtwo(\Lap(z^{\star}))>\vt_1\vt_2,
\quad\text{i.e.}\quad
\lamtwo>\sigmadyn:=\frac{\vt_1\vt_2}{c\,\mF}.
\label{eq:threshold}
\end{equation}
Restricted to a single Fiedler mode, \eqref{eq:jac} reduces to the scalar block
$\dot a=-\mF a+\vt_1 b$, $\dot b=\vt_2 a-c\lamtwo b$, on which $c\,\mF\,\lamtwo>\vt_1\vt_2$
is necessary and sufficient (trace $<0$, determinant $>0$); the decoupled case
$\vt_1\vt_2=0$ is trivially stable.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
With $W=\tfrac12\|\xi\|^2+\tfrac{\vt_1}{2\vt_2}\|\eta\|^2$,
$\dot W\le-\mF\|\xi\|^2-\tfrac{\vt_1}{\vt_2}c\lamtwo\|\eta\|^2+2\vt_1\|\xi\|\|\eta\|$ is
negative definite iff $\mF\,\tfrac{\vt_1}{\vt_2}c\lamtwo>\vt_1^2$, i.e.\
\eqref{eq:threshold}; the scalar Fiedler block gives the converse.
\end{proof}
This is a conditional interconnection result, \emph{not} a convergence proof
for~\eqref{eq:pd}; it concerns the optimizer--estimator (information) layer only, not the
unicycle/load dynamics. It links the connectivity requirement to the information-layer
stability boundary $\sigmadyn$, which the WISE margin
$\sigmastar=\sigmadyn+\delta$ satisfies by construction.

\subsection{Selection cost and prices}
The lexicographic selector of Theorem~\ref{thm:existence} is exact. A scalarised
alternative $V_\varepsilon=V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$ recovers it only in the limit: any
maximiser obeys $0\le V^{\star}-V(z_\varepsilon)\le\varepsilon\operatorname{osc}(\lamtwo)$
and $\|Bz_\varepsilon-y^{\star}\|\le\sqrt{2\varepsilon\operatorname{osc}(\lamtwo)/\alpha}$
(Tikhonov~\cite{facchinei2003fvi}), so a finite $\varepsilon$ carries no exact-preservation
guarantee and can move the aggregate (measurably so in Section~\ref{sec:exp}), whereas the
lexicographic form preserves it exactly. The exact certificate is solved in the lifted
variables $(z,\xi)$ of~\eqref{eq:sdp}; the decentralized heuristic of
Section~\ref{sec:algorithm} instead uses a projected wrench-separation oracle on
$X_{\mathrm f}=\{H_wz\ge d\}$.

With $\LamX=\max_{z\in X_{\mathrm f}}\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\ge\LamE$, the requirement partitions
instances into three regimes: \emph{free} ($\LamE\ge\sigmastar$: connectivity at no
productive cost), \emph{costly} ($\LamE<\sigmastar\le\LamX$: a connectivity-feasible
assignment exists but not a productively optimal one), and \emph{impossible}
($\LamX<\sigmastar$).

\begin{remark}[Finite-population recovery]\label{rem:integer}
The analysis is a fluid relaxation; integer assignments are recovered by rounding and
re-certified numerically (no $O(1/N)$ claim, and best-of-$R$ rounding is a search, not a
certificate). The wrench and connectivity margins $H_wz\ge d+m_W$ and $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$
leave slack, so on the $N=12$ demonstration a single randomized-rounding draw re-certifies
$66\%$ of the seeds and up to $30$ independent draws re-certify all $30$ (argmax rounding
$67\%$); naive rounding of a zero-margin optimum does not.
\end{remark}
