\section{Certificate and Decentralized Realization}
\label{sec:algorithm}

We separate the exact certificate from the online controller: existence and
selection of a WISE are decided by the semidefinite program of
Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}, solved centrally; the primal--dual flow below is a
decentralized realization evaluated empirically, not a convergence guarantee.

\subsection{Exact certificate (centralized SDP)}
Stage 1 fixes $y^{\star}$ by maximising $V$ over $X_{\mathrm f}$; the Stage-2
SDP~\eqref{eq:sdp} then returns $\LamE$ and the most-connected productively optimal
assignment $z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}$, and $\LamE\ge\sigmastar$ is the WISE existence
test (Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}). We report this certificate and use it as a relaxed
reference for the simulations.

\subsection{Decentralized realization}
For an online approximation each type runs a projected primal--dual flow with a
hard-wrench dual $\mu$ and a connectivity dual $\pi$:
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:pd}
\begin{align}
\dot z_\tau &= \mathcal R_\tau\!\Big(z,\;\nabla_{z_\tau}V(z)+H_w^{\top}\mu
+\pi\,\nabla_{z_\tau}\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\Big),\label{eq:pd-x}\\
\dot\mu &= \big[d-H_w z\big]_+^{\mu},\qquad
\dot\pi = \big[\sigmastar-\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\big]_+^{\pi},\label{eq:pd-pi}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
with $\mathcal R_\tau$ a budget-preserving revision protocol. The aggregate deficit
$d-H_w z$ is tracked by dynamic average consensus and $\mu\ge0$ is the wrench dual. The
Fiedler sensitivity $v^{\top}L_e v$ needs the connectivity eigenpair; each robot
estimates $(\lamtwo,v)$ by a deflated power iteration on $\mathbf 1^{\perp}$ over the
current graph, whose error enters $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{est}}$ in $\delta$ (full
recursion in the repository). A missing wrench direction raises $\mu$, missing
connectivity raises the relay price $\pi$, and count migrates toward the highest
marginal type--action value. We use a projected-ascent (Nash-stationary) revision
whose rest points are the constrained maximisers of
$V$~\cite{sandholm2010population}; replicator dynamics are avoided because they cannot
revive an action driven to zero, stalling short of $\Ess$ when a relay type must be
reactivated.

We make no global-convergence claim for~\eqref{eq:pd}: the induced graph is
state-dependent, the flow assumes a connected start, and it is evaluated only in
simulation (Section~\ref{sec:exp}). Existence and selection rest on the exact SDP, not
on the flow.
