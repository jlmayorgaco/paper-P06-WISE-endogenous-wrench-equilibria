\section{Certificate and Decentralized Realization}
\label{sec:algorithm}

We separate the exact certificate from the online controller: existence and
selection of a WISE are decided by the semidefinite program of
Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}, solved centrally; the primal--dual flow below is a
decentralized \emph{heuristic} realization evaluated empirically, not a convergence
guarantee.

\subsection{Exact certificate (centralized SDP)}
Stage 1 fixes $y^{\star}$ by maximising $V$ over $X_{\mathrm f}$; the Stage-2
SDP~\eqref{eq:sdp} then returns $\LamE$ and the most-connected productively optimal
assignment $z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}$, and $\LamE\ge\sigmastar$ is the WISE existence
test (Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}). We report this certificate and use it as a relaxed
reference for the simulations.

\subsection{Decentralized realization}
For an online approximation we realize the flow at the robot level: robot $i$ of type
$\tau$ revises its own action $x_{ia}$ ($z_{\tau a}=\sum_{i\in\tau}x_{ia}$), and the shared
per-site occupancy $\sum_\tau z_{\tau,kh}\le1$ is held by a local occupancy price
$\rho_{kh}$ (one-at-a-time exclusion, so two robots cannot seize a slot). On the projected
representation $X_{\mathrm f}$ the flow uses a hard-wrench dual $\mu$ and a connectivity
dual $\pi$:
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:pd}
\begin{align}
\dot z_\tau &= \mathcal R_\tau\!\Big(z,\;\nabla_{z_\tau}V(z)+H_w^{\top}\mu
-O_\tau^{\top}\rho+\pi\,\nabla_{z_\tau}\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\Big),\label{eq:pd-x}\\
\dot\mu &= \big[d-H_w z\big]_+^{\mu},\qquad
\dot\rho = \big[Oz-\mathbf 1\big]_+^{\rho},\label{eq:pd-mu}\\
\dot\pi &= \big[\sigmastar-\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\big]_+^{\pi},\label{eq:pd-pi}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
where $(Oz)_{kh}=\sum_\tau z_{\tau,kh}$ collects the shared slot- and relay-occupancy so
$\rho\ge0$ prices exclusivity.
with $\mathcal R_\tau$ a budget-preserving projected-ascent revision whose rest points
maximise $V$~\cite{sandholm2010population}. Each robot estimates $(\lamtwo,v)$ by power
iteration on the deflated, shifted operator $P=I-\beta\Lap(z)-\tfrac1N\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\top}$
($0<\beta<1/\lambda_{\max}$, so the Fiedler pair is dominant on $\mathbf1^{\perp}$),
sign-fixed by a reference index; the eigengap $\lambda_3-\lamtwo$ sets the convergence rate
and iteration stops when the eigen-residual $\|Pv-\widehat\mu v\|\le\tau_{\mathrm{eig}}$
($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{est}}$ in $\delta$; recursion and message schedule in the
repository). This is a \emph{preservation}, not a recovery, mechanism: it assumes a
connected warm start $\lamtwo\ge\sigma_{\mathrm{safe}}>0$ and eigengap
$\lambda_3-\lamtwo\ge g_{\min}>0$, on a slower time scale than the estimator. The flow is
integrated in simulation by projected forward Euler
$z^{k+1}=\Pi_{\mathcal X}[z^k+h\,\dot z^k]$ with step $h$. We make no global-convergence
claim for~\eqref{eq:pd}; existence and selection rest on the exact SDP.
