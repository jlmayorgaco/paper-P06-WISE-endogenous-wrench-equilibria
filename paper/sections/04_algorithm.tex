\section{Certificate and Decentralized Realization}
\label{sec:algorithm}

We separate the exact certificate from the online controller: existence and
selection of a WISE are decided by the semidefinite program of
Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}, solved centrally; the primal--dual flow below is a
decentralized \emph{heuristic} realization evaluated empirically, not a convergence
guarantee.

\subsection{Exact certificate (centralized SDP)}
Stage 1 fixes $y^{\star}$ by maximising $V$ over $X_{\mathrm f}$; the Stage-2
SDP~\eqref{eq:sdp} then returns $\LamE$ and the most-connected productively optimal
assignment $z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}$, and $\LamE\ge\sigmastar$ is the WISE existence
test (Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}). We report this certificate and use it as a relaxed
reference for the simulations.

\subsection{Decentralized realization}
We write the online approximation as a \emph{type-population} flow: $z_{\tau a}=\sum_{i\in
\tau}x_{ia}$ is the type aggregate of individual assignments $x_{ia}$, and each robot
samples its own action from the type distribution, so~\eqref{eq:pd} is the aggregate of the
per-robot revisions rather than a controller run by a coordinator. Shared per-site
occupancy $\sum_\tau z_{\tau,kh}\le1$ is priced by $\rho_{kh}\ge0$ (a soft penalty; strict
exclusivity requires a separate one-at-a-time arbitration). The flow carries a hard-wrench
dual $\mu$ and a connectivity dual $\pi$:
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:pd}
\begin{align}
\dot z_\tau &= \mathcal R_\tau\!\Big(z,\;\nabla_{z_\tau}V(z)+H_w^{\top}\mu
-O_\tau^{\top}\rho+\pi\,\nabla_{z_\tau}\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\Big),\label{eq:pd-x}\\
\dot\mu &= \big[d-H_w z\big]_+^{\mu},\qquad
\dot\rho = \big[Oz-\mathbf 1\big]_+^{\rho},\label{eq:pd-mu}\\
\dot\pi &= \big[\sigmastar-\lamtwo(\Lap(z))\big]_+^{\pi},\label{eq:pd-pi}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
with $(Oz)_{kh}=\sum_\tau z_{\tau,kh}$ the shared slot/relay occupancy priced by $\rho\ge0$
and $\mathcal R_\tau$ a budget-preserving projected-ascent revision whose rest points
maximise $V$~\cite{sandholm2010population}. Each robot estimates $(\lamtwo,v)$ by power
iteration on $P=I-\beta\Lap(z)-\tfrac1N\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\top}$ ($0<\beta<1/\lambda_{\max}$),
sign-fixed by a reference index and stopped at eigen-residual
$\|Pv-\widehat\mu v\|\le\tau_{\mathrm{eig}}$ (the eigengap sets the rate;
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{est}}$ in $\delta$; recursion in the repository). This
\emph{preserves} rather than recovers connectivity: it assumes a connected warm start
$\lamtwo\ge\sigma_{\mathrm{safe}}$ and gap $\lambda_3-\lamtwo\ge g_{\min}>0$, on a slower
scale than the estimator; in simulation it is integrated by forward Euler
$z^{k+1}=\Pi_{\mathcal X_0}[z^k+h\,\dot z^k]$ with $\Pi_{\mathcal X_0}$ the projection onto
the \emph{local} per-type budget simplices $\{z\ge0:\sum_a z_{\tau a}=n_\tau\}$ (occupancy
handled by $\rho$, not by a global projection). We make no global-convergence claim;
existence and selection rest on the exact SDP.
