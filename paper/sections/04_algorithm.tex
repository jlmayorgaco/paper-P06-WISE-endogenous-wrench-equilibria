\section{Distributed Algorithm}
\label{sec:algorithm}

We seek a WISE by a projected primal--dual flow with a hard-wrench dual $\mu$ and a
connectivity dual $\pi$:
\begin{subequations}\label{eq:pd}
\begin{align}
\dot x_\tau &= \mathcal R_\tau\!\Big(x,\;\nabla_{x_\tau}V(x)+H_w^{\top}\mu
+\pi\,\nabla_{x_\tau}\lamtwo(\Lap(x))\Big),\label{eq:pd-x}\\
\dot\mu &= \big[d-H_w x\big]_+^{\mu},\qquad
\dot\pi = \big[\sigmastar-\lamtwo(\Lap(x))\big]_+^{\pi},\label{eq:pd-pi}
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
where $\mathcal R_\tau$ is a mass-preserving revision protocol. The aggregate deficit
$d-H_w x$ is obtained by dynamic average consensus; $\mu\ge0$ is the wrench-feasibility
dual. The Fiedler sensitivity $v^{\top}L_e v$ requires the algebraic-connectivity
eigenpair; in this study it is computed \emph{supervisorily} (a decentralized Fiedler
estimator would make the certification fully distributed, and its estimation error
enters $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{est}}$ in $\delta$). A missing wrench direction raises
$\mu$; missing connectivity raises the relay price $\pi$; mass migrates toward the
highest marginal type-action value.

We use a projected-ascent (Nash-stationary) revision, whose rest points are the
constrained maximisers of $V$~\cite{sandholm2010population}. Replicator dynamics are
avoided: they cannot revive an action driven to zero, so on a fiber whose safe
subset needs a re-activated relay type they can stall short of $\Ess$.

\begin{proposition}[Safe connectivity-selection flow]\label{prop:conv}
In the free regime ($\Ess\neq\varnothing$), consider the projection dynamics
$\dot x=\Pi_{T_{\Emanifold}(x)}\!\big[\nabla\lamtwo(\Lap(x))\big]$ started at
$x(0)\in\Emanifold$ with $\lamtwo(\Lap(x(0)))\ge\sigmastar$ and $\lamtwo$ simple
along the arc. Then $\Emanifold$ is forward invariant,
$\tfrac{d}{dt}\lamtwo=\nabla\lamtwo^{\top}\Pi_{T_{\Emanifold}}\nabla\lamtwo\ge0$
(the safety margin never decreases), and trajectories converge to the set of
maximisers of the concave $\lamtwo$ over the convex $\Emanifold$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Tangent-cone projection keeps $x\in\Emanifold$. Along differentiable arcs
$\dot\lamtwo=\|\Pi_{T_{\Emanifold}}\nabla\lamtwo\|^2\ge0$; a rest point satisfies
$\Pi_{T_{\Emanifold}}\nabla\lamtwo=0$, the optimality condition for the concave
$\lamtwo$ on the convex $\Emanifold$. LaSalle with $-\lamtwo$ gives convergence to
the maximiser set (not necessarily a point).
\end{proof}

This is a \emph{safe selection stage}, not a self-bootstrap: it assumes a connected
start in $\Emanifold$ and covers neither initialisation outside the safe region nor
global convergence of the full mobile-robot system. The primal--dual flow
\eqref{eq:pd} is used as a \emph{numerical solver} for $\max_{x\in\Ess}V$; we make
no global-convergence claim for it, and the connectivity certification (eigenpair)
is supervisory rather than fully distributed (Section~\ref{sec:exp}). In the costly
regime the target is $\max_{x\in X_{\mathrm f}\cap\Cdelta}V$, which the neutral flow
over $\Emanifold$ cannot reach.
