\section{Centralized Relaxed Certificate}
\label{sec:algorithm}

Existence and selection of a WISE are decided by the semidefinite program of
Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}, solved centrally over the fluid relaxation. Stage~1 fixes
$y^{\star}$ by maximising $V$ over $X_{\mathrm f}$; the Stage-2 SDP~\eqref{eq:sdp} then
returns $\LamE$ and the most-connected productively optimal assignment
$z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}$, and $\LamE\ge\sigmastar$ is the relaxed WISE existence test
(Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}). We report this certificate and use it as the relaxed
reference for the simulations; an integer team is recovered by rounding and re-certified
against the margins of Proposition~\ref{prop:integer}.

A fully \emph{distributed} realization---in which each robot samples its own action from a
type-population flow using only locally communicated aggregates and duals, and provably
converges to $\Emanifold^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}}$ over the very graph it forms---is the
natural next step, but requires a local message protocol and a convergence guarantee over a
decision-dependent graph that are outside the present relaxed guarantees. We therefore leave
it to future work and claim only the centralized certificate here.
