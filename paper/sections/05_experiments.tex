\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region
world: a rigid load demands a planar wrench, short links connect robots only within a
cluster, and the gap is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The heterogeneity is
\emph{conflicting}: the long-range type has both higher lifting capacity ($c_\ell>c_s$) and
stronger relay gain ($\gamma_\ell>\gamma_s$), so it is the more valuable resource for both
roles and WISE must reorganize a whole composition to free it for the relay it alone bridges.
Integer assignments are embedded in a geometric simulator solely to evaluate the induced
communication graph $\Lgeo(q)$; closed-loop transport dynamics are outside the present
validation. The primary metric is the \emph{certified service rate}: served only when
wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and reproduces
exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Non-trivial composition exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:flagship})}
The two-load flagship instance ($N=12$, $c_L=2c_S$) certifies a genuine comparative-advantage
reorganization: releasing one long lifter for two short lifters and moving it to the relay
holds $Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$, both wrenches, $V=V^{\star}$ and $n_{\mathrm{active}}=6$ fixed,
while $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)$ rises $\approx0\!\to\!0.41$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:freeconn}, $D\lamtwo[d]>0$).
With $\operatorname{rank}[A;B]=14$, $\dim\Emanifold=58$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}); its
certified neutral direction preserves both components of $Bz=(3,3)$ to $<10^{-8}$. It is not
tied to one bridge: a three-region, two-bridge instance frees \emph{two} long robots to two
relays (shorts compensating), holding $(3,3,3)$ while $\lamtwo:0\!\to\!0.21$. On $200$ symmetric
$\lamtwo{=}\lambda_3$ instances the eigenspace criterion attains median \emph{absolute}
finite-difference error $10^{-6}$ with correct sign in $200/200$ cases, versus $0.04$ for an
arbitrary single eigenvector (sign-correct here but quantitatively unreliable). The Weyl
transfer $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\ge\lamtwo(\Lap)-\varepsilon_L$ held on all $2000$ perturbations
($\rho\le0.3$, realized $\varepsilon_{\mathrm{track}}\le0.43$); as the a-priori Lipschitz bound
($6.6$) far exceeds both the realized mismatch and $\lamtwo$, Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge} serves as a
conservative \emph{post-recovery} transfer check ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{model}}$ offline).

\paragraph*{E2 --- Integer recovery and an exact oracle (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer}, Table~\ref{tab:oracle})}
At $N=12$, \emph{structured} recovery (Sec.~\ref{sec:algorithm}) re-certifies an integer
self-sustaining assignment on the \emph{first} draw for all $30$ seeds ($30/30$), versus
$29/30$ (best-of-$30$) for independent rounding and $7/30$ for argmax. Its median productive
loss is $2.3\%$ after the matching and $4.9\%$ after the connectivity repair, while
independent best-of-$30$ reaches $2.4\%$ but recovers a jointly feasible team on only $29/30$
(optimality not claimed). Only $4/30$ structured recoveries satisfy $B\hat z=y^{\star}$
exactly ($\|B\hat z-y^\star\|_\infty\le\tau_B$); the rest stay jointly feasible at that loss.
For $N\le8$ we enumerate
\emph{every} $|\mathcal
A|^N$ robot--action map exactly (Table~\ref{tab:oracle}): at the design requirement no false
positives occur ($0/23$) and the worst-case gap $\max g_{\mathbb Z}=V^\star-V_{\mathbb
Z}^\star\le0.005$ ($<0.06\%$ of $V^\star$; Table~\ref{tab:oracle} reports both). Pushed
\emph{adversarially} to $\sigma=0.97\LamE$
(the fiber ceiling) it is \emph{not} always integer-tight at the smallest $N$: $N{=}6$ gives
$13/50$ boundary false positives (Wilson $[16,40]\%$), and none at $N{=}7,8$ ($0/50$ each,
Wilson upper $7\%$).

\paragraph*{E3 --- Selection among productive optima ($30$ paired seeds)}
Among selectors on the same productively optimal face, the hard connectivity-constrained
optimum ($\max V$ s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) reaches $V^{\star}$ but may return \emph{any}
such point, merely \emph{satisfying} the requirement (margin $+0.15$). WISE is their
lexicographic \emph{tie-break}, selecting the maximum-margin point ($+2.55$; $100\%$ certified,
Wilson $[89,100]$), versus random-fiber $+0.10$ (sometimes infeasible) and least-role-change
$+0.38$. Stage-1 is
$\sim\!10$\,ms; the Stage-2 SDP grows with the $(N{-}1)$-LMI ($29$\,ms$\to$$98$\,s,
$N{=}12{\to}96$; CVXPY~1.9/CLARABEL~0.11, tol $10^{-7}$, single-threaded, hardware logged in
the repository, with $T,M,H,|\mathcal R|$ fixed so this isolates spectral-LMI scaling in $N$,
not full MRTA scaling): offline-ready.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Price of self-sustainability (Thm.~\ref{thm:price}, Fig.~\ref{fig:price})}
Sweeping $\sigma$ traces $\Price(\sigma)$ over $40$ seeds: zero up to $\LamE$ (free), positive
on $(\LamE,\LamX]$ (costly), infeasible beyond (impossible), matching \eqref{eq:trichotomy} on
every seed ($\LamE<\LamX$, median costly width $0.64$). The dual is a genuine price: in
unnormalized $\sigma$, $\pi^{\star}=\operatorname{tr}Y^{\star}$ matches $\Delta\Price/\Delta\sigma$
over $84$ interior points ($r=0.996$, median relative error $0.6\%$; central differences
$\Delta\sigma{=}0.05$ on the interior $25$--$75\%$ of $(\LamE,\LamX)$, no kink straddled),
confirming $\pi^{\star}\in\partial\Price$ at differentiable points (Fig.~\ref{fig:price}
normalization is display-only).
