\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the four results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a
two-region world: a rigid load in the right cluster demands a planar wrench, and
short links connect robots only within a cluster, so the gap is bridged only when
a long-range type relays. The strategic layer runs the primal--dual flow
\eqref{eq:pd}; the physical layer integrates a rigid load and second-order
unicycles, with each lifter's contact force confined to a heading-aligned
elliptical set (nonholonomic: free along the heading, limited laterally), so wrench
realizability depends on pose. Connectivity in the physical layer is
evaluated on the geometric Laplacian $\Lgeo(q)$ induced by the realized poses,
whereas the strategic layer uses only its convex surrogate $\Lap(z)$; the physical
guarantee transfers through the mismatch bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}. For the
representative instance the maximum observed mismatch
$\|\Lgeo(q)-\Lap(z)\|_2$ (at candidate-site tracking radius $\rho$) stays within
the Lipschitz bound $C_L\rho$ that sizes $\varepsilon_L$, and Weyl's bound
\eqref{eq:weyl} holds on every sampled state. The primary metric is the
\emph{certified service rate}: a load counts as served only when it is
wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and
reproduces exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Convergence (Fig.~\ref{fig:conv})}
On a representative instance the distributed flow drives $\lamtwo(\Lap(z))$ to at
least $\sigmastar$ (it settles \emph{above} $\sigmastar$, by the robustness margin)
and the wrench residual to zero together: the equilibrium becomes wrench-feasible
and self-computing at once. The information price $\pi$ settles at its certification
value; in these instances the relay assignment accrues to long-range types.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Self-defeat threshold (Fig.~\ref{fig:thresh}, Thm.~\ref{thm:stability})}
On one instance we compare two compositions from the same fiber $\Emanifold$. When a
long-range robot relays, the induced $\lamtwo(t)$ rises above $\sigmastar$ and the
coupled estimation error $\|e(t)\|$ decays; when it is withdrawn, $\lamtwo(t)$ stays
below and $\|e(t)\|$ grows---the equilibrium defeats its own estimator. The sign
change occurs exactly at $c\,\mF\,\lamtwo=\vt^2$, the derived threshold, not a
fitted curve.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Regime phase diagram (Fig.~\ref{fig:phase}, Thm.~\ref{thm:trichotomy})}
Sweeping the long-range fraction $\nu$ and torque demand $\tau_d$ ($n=12$
seeds/cell), the empirical certification rate collapses in the two regimes the
trichotomy predicts: at $\nu\!\to\!0$ no composition reaches $\LamE\ge\sigmastar$
(impossible/costly), and at high $\tau_d$ the fleet is wrench-limited.

\caption{Empirical certified-service-rate phase diagram over $(\nu,\tau_d)$

the theoretical $\LamE=\sigmastar$ boundary. The trichotomy attributes the two
failure edges to existence ($\nu\!\to\!0$) and wrench feasibility (high $\tau_d$).}

\end{figure}

\paragraph*{E4 --- Constraint ablation (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}, Fig.~\ref{fig:roles}a)}
On paired seeds, enforcing either requirement alone fails: \texttt{scalar\_capacity}
and \texttt{wrench\_only} build valid coalitions that fragment the network,
\texttt{connectivity\_only} keeps the network but cannot lift, and only WISE
certifies both. WISE and the centralized oracle agree on this operating point,
which is a numerical sanity check (both solve the same relaxed program), not a
proof of distributed optimality. Table~\ref{tab:ablation} reports the run count and
$95\%$ paired-bootstrap intervals.

\paragraph*{E5 --- Emergent roles (Fig.~\ref{fig:roles}b, Prop.~\ref{prop:cycle})}
Pooling WISE solutions across seeds, \emph{in the tested instances} relay
commitment is drawn from long-range types. This is the comparative-advantage
mechanism of Proposition~\ref{prop:cycle}: a productively neutral role exchange
between a long- and a short-range type leaves $Bz$ fixed while raising $\lamtwo$
(positive directional derivative), so the connectivity selector concentrates relays
on the long-range types---not a general theorem, but the predicted division of
labour.
