\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a
two-region world: a rigid load in the right cluster demands a planar wrench, and
short links connect robots only within a cluster, so the gap is bridged only when a
long-range type relays. The strategic layer runs~\eqref{eq:pd}; the physical layer
integrates a rigid load and second-order unicycles whose lifting force is confined to
the heading-aligned elliptical set, so wrench realizability depends on pose.
Connectivity is measured on the geometric Laplacian $\Lgeo(q)$ of the realized poses
while the strategic layer uses the surrogate $\Lap(z)$; the maximum observed mismatch
$\|\Lgeo(q)-\Lap(z)\|_2$ stays within the Lipschitz bound $C_L\rho$ that sizes
$\varepsilon_L$, so Weyl's bound~\eqref{eq:weyl} holds on every sampled state
(Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}). The primary metric is the \emph{certified service rate}: a
load counts as served only when it is wrench-feasible \emph{and}
$\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and reproduces exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Convergence and self-defeat (Thm.~\ref{thm:stability})}
The decentralized realization~\eqref{eq:pd} drives $\lamtwo(\Lap(z))$ above
$\sigmastar$ and the wrench residual to zero together, with the connectivity price
$\pi$ settling on long-range relays. Comparing two fiber compositions traces the
boundary of Theorem~\ref{thm:stability}: when a long-range robot relays the estimation
error $\|e(t)\|$ decays, and when it is withdrawn ($\lamtwo<\sigmastar$) it
grows---the subsystem defeats its own estimator, with the sign change at
$c\,\mF\,\lamtwo=\vt^2$ (a dedicated sweep is built in the extended study).

\paragraph*{E3 --- Regime phase diagram (Fig.~\ref{fig:phase}, Thm.~\ref{thm:trichotomy})}
Sweeping the long-range fraction $\nu$ and torque demand $\tau_d$, we solve the WISE
SDP~\eqref{eq:sdp} in every cell and overlay its exact boundary $\LamE=\sigmastar$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:phase}). The two predicted failure edges appear: existence fails as
$\nu\!\to\!0$ (no long-range robot can relay) and wrench feasibility fails at high
$\tau_d$. Against the decentralized flow the SDP verdict has \emph{perfect recall}
over $144$ runs (no false negatives; $61$ TP, $64$ TN, $19$ FP, $0$ FN): its only
misses are cells the exact certificate clears but the heuristic flow does not reach.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Constraint ablation (Table~\ref{tab:ablation})}
On paired seeds, enforcing either requirement alone fails: \texttt{scalar\_capacity}
and \texttt{wrench\_only} fragment the network, \texttt{connectivity\_only} keeps the
network but cannot lift, and only WISE certifies both (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}, run
count and $95\%$ paired-bootstrap intervals). WISE and the centralized oracle agree
here---a numerical sanity check, both solving the same relaxed program, not a proof
of distributed optimality.

\paragraph*{E5 --- Emergent roles (Prop.~\ref{prop:cycle})}
Pooled over seeds, relay commitment falls on long-range types, as
Proposition~\ref{prop:cycle} predicts: a neutral long/short exchange leaves $Bz$ fixed
while raising $\lamtwo$. This is the predicted division of labour in the tested
instances, not a general theorem.
