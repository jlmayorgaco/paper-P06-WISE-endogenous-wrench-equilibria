\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region world where the gap
is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The heterogeneity is \emph{conflicting}: the
long-range type has both higher capacity ($c_\ell>c_s$) and relay gain
($\gamma_\ell>\gamma_s$), so WISE must reorganize a whole composition to free it for the relay
it alone bridges. Integer assignments are embedded in a geometric simulator only to evaluate
$\Lgeo(q)$; closed-loop dynamics are outside this validation. The primary metric is the
\emph{certified service rate} (served only when wrench-feasible \emph{and}
$\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$). All experiments use fixed recorded seeds, and every reported number is written to a
manifest by the script that produces it.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Non-trivial composition exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:flagship})}
The two-load flagship ($N=12$, $c_L=2c_S$) certifies a comparative-advantage reorganization:
releasing one long lifter for two short lifters and moving it to the relay holds
$Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$, both wrenches and $V=V^{\star}$, while $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)$ rises
$\approx0\!\to\!0.41$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:networkability}, $\Gamma_{\Emanifold}>0$). With
$\operatorname{rank}[A;B]=14$, $\dim\Emanifold=58$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}); its neutral
direction preserves both components of $Bz=(3,3)$ to $<10^{-8}$. A three-region, two-bridge
instance similarly frees \emph{two} long robots to two relays, holding $(3,3,3)$ while
$\lamtwo:0\!\to\!0.21$. On $200$ symmetric $\lamtwo{=}\lambda_3$
instances the eigenspace criterion matches the finite-difference derivative to median absolute
error $10^{-6}$ (correct sign $200/200$), versus $0.04$ for a single eigenvector. With
lower-bound Laplacians the transfer is \emph{a priori}: across $10{,}000$ perturbations
($\rho\le0.3$) the geometric graph dominated the surrogate in Loewner order in $100\%$ of
trials, so $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\ge\lamtwo(\Lap)$ held with minimum spectral margin $0.0017$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}); as the surrogate is built from tube infima this is a construction
check, not an independent test of Assumption~\ref{as:standing}(iv).

\paragraph*{E1b --- The networkability certificate is global ($12$ seeds)}
On $12$ instances we evaluate $\Gamma_{\Emanifold}$ at an interior fiber point $\bar z$ by the
eigenspace SDP~\eqref{eq:gamma} and compare with $\LamE$ from the Stage-2 SDP. The
local--global equivalence~\eqref{eq:iff} holds on $12/12$; at the selector the certificate
vanishes, $\Gamma_{\Emanifold}(z^{\star}_{\mathrm{WISE}})\le3.4\cdot10^{-6}$ versus
$\Gamma_{\Emanifold}(\bar z)\in[0.45,1.78]$ (ratio $\le6.3\cdot10^{-6}$), and the concavity
bound~\eqref{eq:gammabound} holds on $12/12$. The closed form $\|\Pi_{N_p}g_\lambda\|_2$ and
the general SDP agree to $2\cdot10^{-12}$, so \eqref{eq:dirderiv} unifies the simple and
repeated cases. Crucially, degeneracy and \emph{usable} degeneracy differ by an order of
magnitude (Remark~\ref{rem:dnet}): $\dim\Emanifold=59$ on these instances while
$d_{\mathrm{net}}\in[10,12]$---most productively neutral directions are invisible to the
communication layer.

\paragraph*{E5 --- Information-layer stability at zero productive cost (Fig.~\ref{fig:modal})}
Reading $\lamtwo$ from the flagship record and using the scenario's own gains
($\sigmadyn=\vt_1\vt_2/(c\mF)=0.25$), the two productively identical assignments fall on
opposite sides of the threshold. The productive-only optimum has $\lamtwo=4.4\cdot10^{-16}$,
so $\det A_{\lamtwo}=-0.25<0$ and $\alpha=-0.207$: the information layer \emph{diverges},
growing $461\times$ over the horizon. WISE has $\lamtwo=0.415>\sigmadyn$, giving
$\det A_{\lamtwo}=+0.165$ and a certified rate $\alpha=+0.128$, with
$\|e(T)\|/\|e(0)\|=2.1\cdot10^{-2}$. The analytic rate~\eqref{eq:rate} matches the observed
exponential slope to $5.6\cdot10^{-17}$ (exact matrix exponential, so no integrator error is
mistaken for dynamics). This is the reduced information layer only---no robot, load or
actuator dynamics, and no closed-loop transport claim---but it is the property $\sigmastar$ was
introduced to secure, obtained here at $\Delta V=0$.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Integer recovery and an exact oracle (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer}, Table~\ref{tab:oracle})}
At $N=12$, \emph{structured} recovery (Sec.~\ref{sec:algorithm}) is robust across random
processing orders: over $30$ instances $\times\,30$ independent draws ($900$ recoveries),
direct rounding is jointly feasible in $653/900$ ($72.6\%$), and the one-step repair (mean
$0.27$, max $1$ move) raises this to $895/900$. The $30$ draws of an instance share its
problem, so the \emph{instance} is the experimental unit: the mean of the $30$ per-instance
rates is $99.4\%$ with cluster-bootstrap $95\%$ CI $[98.3,100.0]$ ($20{,}000$ resamples of
instances), per-instance median $100\%$ (IQR $[100,100]$, min $83.3\%$), and $30/30$ instances
succeed on $\ge1$ draw (optimality not claimed). A pooled Wilson interval $[98.7,99.8]$ would
treat the nested draws as independent and is anticonservative. The
relative recovery loss is median $4.2\%$ (IQR $[1.7,8.8]\%$, max $27\%$); $83/895$ ($9.3\%$)
recoveries are exactly zero-cost ($\le\tau_B$)---a property of this fast heuristic, not of the
attainable integer optimum, which the oracle below bounds separately. A loss-budgeted repair
requiring $V(\hat z)\ge(1{-}\eta_{\max})V^{\star}$ still recovers $514/900$ draws at
$\eta_{\max}{=}5\%$ and $806/900$ at $10\%$ (both $29/30$ instances). The worst-case
radius~\eqref{eq:intmargin} is far more conservative than direct re-certification: writing
$\chi=\|\hat z-z^{\star}\|_2/r$ for the ratio of the achieved rounding distance to the
certified radius, $\chi$ has median $191$. In a separate single-recovery-per-seed run (one
recovery from the relaxed optimum, not the best of $30$ orders), $0/30$ seeds are certified by
the radius of Proposition~\ref{prop:integer} while $29/30$ pass direct re-certification; the
$30/30$ above refers to instances succeeding on $\ge1$ of their $30$ draws (the row-normalized
radius $r_w=\min_j(h_j^{\top}z^{\star}-d_j)/\|h_j\|_2$ changes nothing here: no wrench facet is
active, and the spectral term $m_\lambda/\kappa_L$ binds). Proposition~\ref{prop:integer} is
therefore a worst-case certificate, not the operative test. For $N\le8$ we enumerate
\emph{every} $|\mathcal A|^N$ map (Table~\ref{tab:oracle}): at the design requirement no false
positives occur ($0/23$) and $\max g_{\mathbb Z}=V^\star-V_{\mathbb Z}^\star\le0.005$
($<0.06\%$ of $V^\star$). Pushed \emph{adversarially} to $\sigma=0.97\LamE$ it is \emph{not}
always integer-tight at the smallest $N$: $N{=}6$ gives $13/50$ false positives
(Wilson $[16,40]\%$), none at $N{=}7,8$.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Selection among productive optima ($30$ paired seeds)}
On the evaluated free-regime instances, the hard connectivity-constrained optimum ($\max V$
s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) reaches $V^{\star}$ but returns \emph{any} point of that optimal
face, merely \emph{satisfying} it (margin $+0.15$). WISE is their lexicographic
\emph{tie-break}, selecting the maximum-margin point ($+2.55$; relaxed joint feasibility in
$30/30$, Wilson $[89,100]$), versus random-fiber $+0.10$ (sometimes infeasible) and
least-role-change $+0.38$. Stage-1 is $\sim\!10$\,ms; the
Stage-2 SDP grows with the $(N{-}1)$-LMI ($29$\,ms$\to$$98$\,s, $N{=}12{\to}96$;
CVXPY~1.9/CLARABEL~0.11, tol $10^{-7}$, single-threaded, no warm starts, $T,M,H,|\mathcal R|$
fixed so this isolates spectral-LMI scaling in $N$, not full MRTA).

\paragraph*{E6 --- Why lexicographic and not a weighted sum ($8$ free-regime seeds)}
Solving $\max_z V(z)+\varepsilon\lamtwo(\Lap(z))$ as a global SDP over the same feasible set
and sweeping $\varepsilon\in[10^{-3},1]$ separates the two failure modes of scalarisation.
The aggregate drift $\|Bz_\varepsilon-y^{\star}\|$ is nonzero at \emph{every} tested
$\varepsilon$ and grows essentially linearly ($2\cdot10^{-4}$ at $\varepsilon=10^{-3}$,
$0.029$ at $0.1$, $0.284$ at $1$), while the productive loss stays numerically zero until
$\varepsilon\approx0.1$ and reaches $4.7\%$ only at $\varepsilon=1$. The asymmetry is the
point: at a maximiser $V$ is flat to \emph{second} order but the aggregate moves to
\emph{first} order, so a small $\varepsilon$ buys apparent value-preservation while already
displacing $y^{\star}$---and the displacement is invisible in $V$. The lexicographic selector
attains $\|Bz_{\mathrm{WISE}}-y^{\star}\|=0$ and $V^{\star}-V=0$ exactly, with no weight to
calibrate.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Price of self-sustainability (Thm.~\ref{thm:price}, Fig.~\ref{fig:price})}
Sweeping $\sigma$ traces $\Price(\sigma)$ over $40$ seeds: zero up to $\LamE$, positive on
$(\LamE,\LamX]$, infeasible beyond, matching \eqref{eq:trichotomy} on every seed (median costly
width $0.64$). The dual is the local sensitivity of optimal productive loss: in
unnormalized $\sigma$, $\pi^{\star}=\operatorname{tr}Y^{\star}$ matches $\Delta\Price/\Delta\sigma$
over $84$ interior points ($r=0.996$, median error $0.6\%$; $\Delta\sigma{=}0.05$ on the
interior $25$--$75\%$ of $(\LamE,\LamX)$), confirming $\pi^{\star}\in\partial\Price$.
