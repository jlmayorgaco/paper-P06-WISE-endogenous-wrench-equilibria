\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region
world: a rigid load demands a planar wrench, short links connect robots only within a
cluster, and the gap is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The lifters are
second-order unicycles and the load a planar rigid body,
\begin{equation}
\dot p_i=v_i[\cos\theta_i,\sin\theta_i]^{\!\top}\!,\;
m_i\dot v_i\!=\!u_{v,i}\!-\!d_iv_i,\;J_i\dot\omega_i\!=\!u_{\omega,i}\!-\!d_{\omega i}\omega_i,
\label{eq:unicycle}
\end{equation}
with load wrench $\sum_{k,h}A_{kh}f_{i}$ from the admissible contact forces $f_i\in
U_\tau(\theta_i)$. The primary metric is the \emph{certified service rate}: served only
when wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and
reproduces exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Fiber neutrality, certified (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}a)}
At an interior optimum $\mathcal N_p=\ker A\cap\ker B$ has dimension $72-13=59$; moving along
it holds $V,Bz$ at machine precision ($<10^{-8}$) while $\lamtwo$ crosses $\sigmastar$, so the
connectivity-feasible subset lies strictly inside the fiber.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Comparative-advantage exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}b,c)}
A matched long/short pair swaps roles at fixed $Bz=y^{\star}$, $V^{\star}$ and zero wrench
residual: the productively optimal composition (long lifts, short relays) leaves the clusters
unbridged ($\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\approx0$), whereas WISE (short lifts, long relays) bridges them
($\lamtwo(\Lgeo)=0.78$); only connectivity differs.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Lexicographic vs.\ scalarized selection}
On free-regime seeds $V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$ trades productive value for connectivity (loss
$0\!\to\!0.049$, drift $0\!\to\!0.307$ as $\varepsilon:10^{-3}\!\to\!1$), whereas the
lexicographic selector holds both at zero---a formulational, not empirical, gap.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Integer recovery (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer})}
Over $30$ seeds a single randomized-rounding draw re-certifies $66\%$ of fluid optima,
best-of-$30$ all $30$ (argmax $67\%$); welfare gap
$V_{\mathrm{relax}}-V_{\mathrm{int}}=+1.8\%$ (mean), all robots integer.

\paragraph*{E5 --- Selection among productive optima (Table~\ref{tab:ablation})}
Single-criterion baselines rarely clear both tests. The hard connectivity-constrained
optimum ($\max V$ s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) also reaches $V^{\star}$, but in the free
regime it may return \emph{any} point of the optimal face and merely \emph{satisfies} the
requirement (margin $+0.15$). WISE is not a competitor to it but its lexicographic
\emph{tie-break}: among the same connectivity-constrained productive optima it selects the
maximum-margin point ($+0.99$). The relaxed SDP solves in $30$--$60$\,ms at $N=12$.

\paragraph*{E6 --- Price of self-sustainability (Thm.~\ref{thm:price}, Fig.~\ref{fig:price})}
Sweeping $\sigma$ traces $\Price(\sigma)$ over six seeds: zero up to $\LamE$ (free), positive
on $(\LamE,\LamX]$ (costly), infeasible beyond (impossible), matching \eqref{eq:trichotomy} on
every seed ($\LamE<\LamX$, so the costly branch is nonempty). The subgradient
$\pi^{\star}\in\partial\Price$ prices one unit of connectivity.

\paragraph*{E7 --- Information-layer self-defeat (motivating)}
Consistent with the motivating interpretation (Prop.~\ref{thm:stability}), when WISE holds
$\lamtwo(\Lgeo^{P})$ above $\sigmastar$ the coupled optimizer--estimator error decays, whereas
withdrawing the relay drops $\lamtwo$ below $\sigmadyn=\vt_1\vt_2/(c\,\mF)$ and the same error
diverges---the decision would defeat its own computation. This is an information-layer
illustration; closed-loop pose, wrench and actuator validation is future work.
