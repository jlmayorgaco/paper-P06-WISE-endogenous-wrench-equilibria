\paragraph*{E1 --- Mechanism, scope and networkability (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}(a))}
Replacing one long lifter by two short ones and sending the released long robot to a relay site
holds $Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$ and $V=V^{\star}$ exactly, with both nominal wrench certificates
granted by the inner-zonotope LP~\eqref{eq:tensor}, while $\lamtwo(\Lap)$ rises $0\to0.399$ at
no productive cost. No rounding is needed: exhaustive enumeration of all
$|\mathcal A|^N=12^6=2.99\cdot10^6$ maps leaves $3864$ wrench-feasible, $1032$ on $\Emanifold$,
and $0.399$ is the \emph{integer} fiber capacity
$\LamE^{\mathbb Z}=\max_{\Emanifold\cap\{0,1\}^n}\lamtwo(\Lap)$: an optimum, not a recovery.
\emph{Scope.} The flagship's tube infima depend on \emph{both} endpoints' actions, so its
$\Lap$ is bilinear and the instance lies \emph{outside} Assumption~\ref{as:standing}(ii),
Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability} and \eqref{eq:sdp}; forcing action-independent infima instead
gates two relay links just outside range ($5.20$ against $R_L=5.0$) and disconnects
\emph{every} assignment, so we report $N{=}6$ as an exact integer illustration and validate the
affine theory on the $N{=}12$ instances. Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge} and
Cors.~\ref{cor:tv},~\ref{cor:budget} hold at a \emph{fixed} assignment and are unaffected.
There $\dim\Emanifold=59$ and, on $12$ seeds, \eqref{eq:iff} holds $12/12$
($\Gamma_{\Emanifold}\le3.4\cdot10^{-6}$ at the selector against $[0.45,1.78]$ at an interior
fiber point, no inequality active); over $30$ paired seeds the SDP selector re-certifies
$100\%$ (Wilson $[89,100]$) at $V=V^{\star}$ against $0\%$ ($[0,11]$) for wrench-only,
connectivity-only and scalar-capacity selectors. On $200$ symmetric $\lamtwo{=}\lambda_3$
instances the eigenspace criterion beats a single-eigenvector gradient by $4\cdot10^{4}$ in
median error against the finite difference.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Planar closed-loop flagship (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}(b)--(d))}
\emph{Setup.} Two rigid loads with $M_k\dot\nu_k+D_k\nu_k=\sum_{i\in\mathcal C_k}A_{kh(i)}f_i
+d_k(t)$ follow separate curved paths and must resynchronize after a prescribed drag
disturbance. At each $20$\,Hz step a trajectory-PD wrench demand is met by a minimum-effort
allocator restricted to the \emph{same} $U_{\tau(i)}^{\mathrm{in}}$ the certificate uses, while
$\Lgeo(q(t))$, rebuilt from the simulated poses, drives \eqref{eq:interconnection}, whose state
shifts each coalition's path-progress command, an illustrative downstream realization. PROD,
the hard-threshold HARD (cheapest relay site clearing $\sigmastar$, declared tie-break) and
WISE share every other setting, so only the assignment differs.

\emph{Certified.} All three hold $V=V^{\star}$ and both nominal wrench certificates, differing
only in reserve. As $\lamtwo(L_0)=0$ here, \eqref{eq:budget} reads
$\rho_{\mathrm{cert}}=1-\sigmastar/\lamtwo(\Lap)$, so WISE buys twice HARD's certified
degradation reserve at zero loss of $V$
(Table~\ref{tab:glance}). Cor.~\ref{cor:budget} is proved for the affine model but evaluated at
a \emph{fixed} assignment, which is why it applies verbatim here; these percentages are
flagship numbers, not $N{=}12$ ones.

\emph{Observed.} On a predeclared $0.01$ grid of $L_{ir}(\rho)=(1{-}\rho)L_{ir}$ with
assignments, gains and everything else fixed, both stay certified past their budgets and first
fail at $\rho=0.12$ and $0.28$---consistent with \eqref{eq:budget} being a lower bound.
Feasibility is re-checked at \emph{every} control step: outside the disturbance window the demand is met to
$6\cdot10^{-8}$; inside it the team saturates (slack $0$, peak residual $0.82$\,N) and the
governor slows the load. Geometric transfer holds \emph{conditionally on the tubes}: over $9.6\cdot10^{5}$ operational
samples $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)-\lamtwo(\Lap(\hat z))$ has median $+0.065$ and minimum $+0.031$ with
zero tube exits, though the controller does not enforce tube invariance. The certified layer decays at fitted rates $0.156$ (WISE) and $0.108$ (HARD), both
above $\alpha(\sigmastar)=0.040$ (Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}), whereas PROD \emph{grows} by
$5.3\cdot10^{5}$. Over $30$ paired worlds (mass $\pm10\%$, damping $\pm15\%$, initial pose,
disturbance amplitude and onset; the seed/world is the unit) HARD and WISE both succeed
$30/30$ against $0/30$ for PROD and for a random fiber point. WISE's paired median peak
synchronization error is a reduction of $0.733$ $[0.713,0.749]$ against PROD but
$+1.7\cdot10^{-4}$ $[-2.5,5.5]\cdot10^{-4}$ against HARD, i.e.\ indistinguishable. WISE's benefit is therefore
not nominal completion but the larger zero-loss reserve it converts into certified degradation
tolerance and faster information recovery. This simulates the modeled layers, not a nonlinear
robot--load theorem.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Integer recovery and an exact oracle}
The \emph{attainable} integer optimum and the \emph{heuristic} recovery loss are distinct
quantities. For the first, on $N\le8$ instances an exact
oracle enumerates \emph{every} $|\mathcal A|^N$ map and finds an integer self-sustaining gap
below $0.06\%$ of $V^{\star}$, no false positives at the design requirement ($0/23$); pushed
\emph{adversarially} to $\sigma=0.97\LamE$ it is integer-tight at $N{=}6$ in $13/50$ cases,
never at $N{=}7,8$. For the second, at $N=12$ structured rounding is
jointly feasible in $895/900$ draws after repair with median loss $4.2\%$, a property of the
heuristic rather than of the integer optimum. Draws are nested within instances, so the per-instance
rate is $99.4\%$ (cluster bootstrap $[98.3,100.0]$).

\paragraph*{E4 --- Regimes, price and scalarization}
Sweeping $\nu\in[0.17,0.67]$ against $\tau_d\in[1,4]$ ($6\times6$ cells, $4$ seeds) activates
no wrench facet, so $\LamE$ is invariant along $\tau_d$ at fixed $(\nu,\text{seed})$ (spread
$\le2\cdot10^{-8}$) while its per-$\nu$ mean rises $0.82\to2.48$. At $\tau_d=8$ the first
wrench facet binds, preserving the local fiber dimension but shortening its reach; over $40$
further seeds $\Price$ traces the predicted shape, its dual matching the finite difference
($r=0.996$). We additionally compare two controls: the connectivity-constrained optimum reaches
$V^{\star}$ but returns \emph{any} point of that face ($m_\lambda=+0.15$ against WISE's
$+2.55$ on these $N{=}12$ instances, a different scale from the flagship's
Table~\ref{tab:glance}), and a scalarized $V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$ drifts the aggregate by
$2\cdot10^{-4}$ to $0.284$ across $\varepsilon\in[10^{-3},1]$: WISE needs no weight.
