\label{sec:exp}

\paragraph*{E1 --- Affine model: the WISE SDP and its certificate ($N{=}12$)}
Relay sites here are anchored and every relay-gated link uses a precomputed tube containing
all admissible receiver positions, so each $L_{ir}$ is fixed before optimization and
independent of the other robots' actions: Assumption~\ref{as:standing}(ii) holds exactly. With
$n=72$ and $\dim\Emanifold=59$ (residuals $<10^{-15}$), Table~\ref{tab:glance}A reports the
local--global equivalence, the modulus at the selector, direct re-certification at
$V=V^{\star}$, and the margin $m_\lambda$ that separates WISE from the hard-threshold selector
\emph{inside} the model the theorem covers.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Pair-dependent integer stress test ($N{=}6$, Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}(a))}
Here tube infima depend on \emph{both} endpoints' actions, so the induced graph $\Lpair(z)$ is
\emph{not} affine in $z$: this instance lies outside Assumption~\ref{as:standing}(ii),
Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability} and \eqref{eq:sdp}, and no claim below invokes them. Replacing one long lifter by two short ones and sending the released
long robot to a relay site holds $Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$ and $V=V^{\star}$ exactly, with both
nominal wrench certificates granted by \eqref{eq:tensor}, while $\lamtwo(\Lpair)$ rises
$0\to0.399$ at no loss of $V$. Exhaustive enumeration of all $|\mathcal A|^N=12^6=2.99\cdot10^6$
maps leaves $3864$ wrench-feasible and $1032$ on $\Emanifold$, and proves the \emph{integer}
capacity $\Lambda^{\mathbb Z}_{\Emanifold,\mathrm{pair}}
=\max\{\lamtwo(\Lpair(z)):z\in\Emanifold\cap\{0,1\}^n\}=0.3989$; it does not bound the relaxed
affine $\LamE$. For this geometry the action-independent lower bound removes the two discriminatory relay
links (worst-case separation $5.20$ against the $5.0$ range), leaving every clearing assignment
at one common $\lamtwo$, so HARD and WISE coincide.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Planar closed-loop transport (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}(b),(c))}
\emph{Setup.} Two rigid loads with $M_k\dot\nu_k+D_k\nu_k=\sum_{i\in\mathcal C_k}A_{kh(i)}f_i
+d_k(t)$ follow curved paths and must resynchronize after a prescribed drag disturbance. At
each $20$\,Hz step a trajectory-PD wrench demand is met by a minimum-effort allocator
restricted to the \emph{same} $U_{\tau(i)}^{\mathrm{in}}$ the certificate uses, while
$\Lgeo(q(t))$, rebuilt from the simulated poses, drives \eqref{eq:interconnection}, whose
state sets each coalition's progress rate
$\dot s_k=v_0h_k(t)\,\mathrm{mean}_{i\in\mathcal C_k}\mathrm{sat}_{[0,2]}(u_0-k_a[Qa]_i)$ with
$v_0=1/48$\,s$^{-1}$, $u_0=k_a=1$ and $h_k$ a first-order progress governor. Both selectors are exact optima over the
same enumerated integer fiber $\Emanifold_{\mathbb Z}$, declared before the sweep:
$z_{\mathrm{HARD}}\in\arg\min\{c_{\mathrm{role}}(z,z^{\mathrm{prod}}):z\in\Emanifold_{\mathbb Z},
\lamtwo(\Lpair(z))\ge\sigmastar\}$ and
$z_{\text{pair-WISE}}\in\arg\max_{\Emanifold_{\mathbb Z}}\lamtwo(\Lpair(z))$, where
$c_{\mathrm{role}}$ counts role changes from $z^{\mathrm{prod}}$, ties broken by deployment
cost then action index. All else is shared.

\emph{Certified.} All three hold $V=V^{\star}$ and both nominal wrench certificates, differing
only in reserve. Cor.~\ref{cor:budget} applies here even though $\Lpair$ is not affine in $z$,
because it needs only the attenuation path $\Lpair^{\rho}=(1{-}\rho)\Lpair+\rho L_0$ to be
affine in $\rho$ at a \emph{fixed} assignment. With $\lamtwo(L_0)=0$ it reads
$\rho_{\mathrm{cert}}=1-\sigmastar/\lamtwo(\Lpair)$, giving $10.8\%$ for HARD against $24.8\%$
for WISE (Table~\ref{tab:glance}).

\emph{Observed.} On the predeclared $0.01$ grid of $\Lpair^{\rho}$ with assignments, gains and
everything else fixed, the observed curves stay above $\sigmastar$ beyond those certified lower
bounds: HARD through $\rho=0.11$ and WISE through $\rho=0.27$, first falling below at
$\rho=0.12$ and $0.28$. Feasibility is re-checked at \emph{every} control step: the demand is
met to $6\cdot10^{-8}$ outside the disturbance window, and inside it the team saturates (peak
residual $0.82$\,N) while the governor slows the load. Geometric transfer holds
\emph{conditionally on the tubes}: over $9.6\cdot10^{5}$ samples
$\lamtwo(\Lgeo)-\lamtwo(\Lpair(\hat z))$ has median $+0.065$ and minimum $+0.031$ with zero
tube exits, though the controller does not enforce invariance. The information layer decays at
observed fitted rates $0.156$ (WISE) and $0.108$ (HARD), both above $\alpha(\sigmastar)=0.0397$
(Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}), whereas PROD \emph{grows} by $5.3\cdot10^{5}$. Over $30$ paired worlds
(mass $\pm10\%$, damping $\pm15\%$, initial pose, disturbance amplitude and onset; the
seed/world is the unit, all parametric variations of the \emph{same} structural scenario)
HARD and WISE both succeed $30/30$ against $0/30$ for PROD and for a
random fiber point. WISE's paired median peak synchronization error is a reduction of $0.733$
$[0.713,0.749]$ against PROD but $+1.7\cdot10^{-4}$ $[-2.5,5.5]\cdot10^{-4}$ against HARD, so
the two are indistinguishable at mission level; what WISE adds is the larger certified
degradation reserve and the faster information-layer decay.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Integer recovery, oracle, regimes and price ($N{=}12$ unless stated)}
On the tested $N\le8$ instances an exact oracle enumerates \emph{every} $|\mathcal A|^N$ map,
the maximum integer self-sustaining gap is below $0.06\%$ of $V^{\star}$ and no false positive
occurs at the design requirement ($0/23$); at $\sigma=0.97\LamE$ relaxed feasibility fails to
imply integer feasibility in $13/50$ tested $N{=}6$ instances and in $0/50$ at $N{=}7,8$.
Separately, at $N=12$ structured rounding is jointly feasible in $895/900$ draws after repair
with median loss $4.2\%$, a per-instance rate of $99.4\%$ under a cluster bootstrap treating
the instance, not the draw, as the unit. Sweeping the long-range-type fraction
$\nu\in[0.17,0.67]$ against the wrench-demand tightness $\tau_d\in[1,4]$ ($6\times6$ cells,
$4$ seeds) activates no wrench facet, so $\LamE$ is invariant along $\tau_d$ while its
per-$\nu$ mean rises $0.82\to2.48$; at $\tau_d=8$ the first facet binds and $\Price$ traces
the predicted shape, its dual matching the finite difference ($r=0.996$). A scalarized
$V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$ drifts the aggregate by up to $0.284$ over
$\varepsilon\in[10^{-3},1]$.
