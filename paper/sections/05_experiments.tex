\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region world where the gap
is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The heterogeneity is \emph{conflicting}: the
long-range type has both higher capacity ($c_\ell>c_s$) and relay gain
($\gamma_\ell>\gamma_s$), so WISE must reorganize a whole composition to free it for the relay
it alone bridges. Integer assignments are embedded in a geometric simulator only to evaluate
$\Lgeo(q)$; closed-loop dynamics are outside this validation. The primary metric is the
\emph{certified service rate} (served only when wrench-feasible \emph{and}
$\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$); every instance is seeded and reproduces exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Non-trivial composition exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:flagship})}
The two-load flagship ($N=12$, $c_L=2c_S$) certifies a comparative-advantage reorganization:
releasing one long lifter for two short lifters and moving it to the relay holds
$Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$, both wrenches and $V=V^{\star}$, while $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)$ rises
$\approx0\!\to\!0.41$ (Prop.~\ref{prop:freeconn}, $D\lamtwo[d]>0$). With
$\operatorname{rank}[A;B]=14$, $\dim\Emanifold=58$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}); its neutral
direction preserves both components of $Bz=(3,3)$ to $<10^{-8}$. A three-region, two-bridge
instance similarly frees \emph{two} long robots to two relays, holding $(3,3,3)$ while
$\lamtwo:0\!\to\!0.21$. On $200$ symmetric $\lamtwo{=}\lambda_3$
instances the eigenspace criterion matches the finite-difference derivative to median absolute
error $10^{-6}$ (correct sign $200/200$), versus $0.04$ for a single eigenvector. With
lower-bound Laplacians the transfer is \emph{a priori}: across $10{,}000$ perturbations
($\rho\le0.3$) the geometric graph dominated the surrogate in Loewner order in $100\%$ of
trials, so $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\ge\lamtwo(\Lap)$ held with minimum spectral margin $0.0017$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}).

\paragraph*{E2 --- Integer recovery and an exact oracle (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer}, Table~\ref{tab:oracle})}
At $N=12$, \emph{structured} recovery (Sec.~\ref{sec:algorithm}) is robust across random
processing orders: over $30$ instances $\times\,30$ independent draws ($900$ recoveries),
direct rounding is jointly feasible in $653/900$ ($72.6\%$), and the one-step repair (mean
$0.27$, max $1$ move) raises this to $895/900$ ($99.4\%$, Wilson $[98.7,99.8]$), every instance
succeeding on $\ge1$ of its $30$ draws (optimality not claimed). The
relative recovery loss is median $4.2\%$ (IQR $[1.7,8.8]\%$, max $27\%$); $83/895$ ($9.3\%$)
recoveries are exactly zero-cost ($\le\tau_B$). A loss-budgeted repair capping the drop at
$(1{-}\eta_{\max})V^{\star}$ still recovers $514/900$ draws within $\eta_{\max}{=}5\%$ and
$806/900$ within $10\%$ (both $29/30$ instances). For $N\le8$ we enumerate
\emph{every} $|\mathcal A|^N$ map (Table~\ref{tab:oracle}): at the design requirement no false
positives occur ($0/23$) and $\max g_{\mathbb Z}=V^\star-V_{\mathbb Z}^\star\le0.005$
($<0.06\%$ of $V^\star$). Pushed \emph{adversarially} to $\sigma=0.97\LamE$ it is \emph{not}
always integer-tight at the smallest $N$: $N{=}6$ gives $13/50$ false positives
(Wilson $[16,40]\%$), none at $N{=}7,8$.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Selection among productive optima ($30$ paired seeds)}
On the evaluated free-regime instances, the hard connectivity-constrained optimum ($\max V$
s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) reaches $V^{\star}$ but returns \emph{any} point of that optimal
face, merely \emph{satisfying} it (margin $+0.15$). WISE is their lexicographic
\emph{tie-break}, selecting the maximum-margin point ($+2.55$; relaxed joint feasibility in
$30/30$, Wilson $[89,100]$), versus random-fiber $+0.10$ (sometimes infeasible) and
least-role-change $+0.38$. Stage-1 is $\sim\!10$\,ms; the
Stage-2 SDP grows with the $(N{-}1)$-LMI ($29$\,ms$\to$$98$\,s, $N{=}12{\to}96$;
CVXPY~1.9/CLARABEL~0.11, tol $10^{-7}$, single-threaded, no warm starts, $T,M,H,|\mathcal R|$
fixed so this isolates spectral-LMI scaling in $N$, not full MRTA).

\paragraph*{E4 --- Price of self-sustainability (Thm.~\ref{thm:price}, Fig.~\ref{fig:price})}
Sweeping $\sigma$ traces $\Price(\sigma)$ over $40$ seeds: zero up to $\LamE$, positive on
$(\LamE,\LamX]$, infeasible beyond, matching \eqref{eq:trichotomy} on every seed (median costly
width $0.64$). The dual is a genuine price: in
unnormalized $\sigma$, $\pi^{\star}=\operatorname{tr}Y^{\star}$ matches $\Delta\Price/\Delta\sigma$
over $84$ interior points ($r=0.996$, median error $0.6\%$; $\Delta\sigma{=}0.05$ on the
interior $25$--$75\%$ of $(\LamE,\LamX)$), confirming $\pi^{\star}\in\partial\Price$.
