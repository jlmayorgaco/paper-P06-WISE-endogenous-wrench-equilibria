\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region
world: a rigid load demands a planar wrench, short links connect robots only within a
cluster, and the gap is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The heterogeneity is
\emph{conflicting}: the long-range type has both higher lifting capacity ($c_\ell>c_s$) and
stronger relay gain ($\gamma_\ell>\gamma_s$), so it is the more valuable resource for both
roles and WISE must reorganize a whole composition to free it for the relay it alone bridges.
Integer assignments are embedded in a geometric simulator solely to evaluate the induced
communication graph $\Lgeo(q)$; closed-loop transport dynamics are outside the present
validation. The primary metric is the \emph{certified service rate}: served only when
wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and reproduces
exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Non-trivial composition exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:flagship})}
The flagship instance ($N=12$, $c_L=2c_S$) certifies a genuine comparative-advantage
reorganization, not a swap of equals: releasing one long lifter, compensating its capacity
with two short lifters, and moving it to the relay holds $Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$, both wrenches
(inner-polygon LP), $V=V^{\star}$ and $n_{\mathrm{active}}=6$ (two long, four short) exactly
fixed, while $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)$ rises from $\approx0$ to $0.41$---materializing
Prop.~\ref{prop:freeconn} with $D\lamtwo[d]>0$; the fiber itself has $\dim\mathcal N_p=59$
with $V,Bz$ invariant to $<10^{-8}$ (\texttt{exp\_fiber}). Two checks: on a $C_3$-symmetric
graph with $\lamtwo=\lambda_3$ an arbitrary single-vector gradient mispredicts $D\lamtwo$ by
$\sim\!10^{8}{\times}$ while the eigenspace criterion tracks it to $0.6\%$; and the a-priori
bound $4d_{\max}L_\psi\rho$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}) holds at every $\rho$ ($\varepsilon_L$
median $\le0.05$ for $\rho\le0.05$\,m).

\paragraph*{E2 --- Integer recovery and an exact oracle (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer}, Table~\ref{tab:oracle})}
At $N=12$, \emph{structured} recovery---occupancy-aware dependent rounding with a greedy
neutral repair that frees marginal lifters to relay---directly re-certifies an integer
self-sustaining assignment on the \emph{first} draw for all $30$ seeds ($30/30$), versus
$29/30$ (best-of-$30$) for independent rounding and $7/30$ for argmax, trading a modest
rounding loss ($4.5\%$ vs $2.4\%$; optimality is not claimed). For $N\le8$ we enumerate
\emph{every} $|\mathcal
A|^N$ robot--action map exactly (Table~\ref{tab:oracle}): at the design requirement no false
positives occur ($0/23$) and the worst-case gap $\max g_{\mathbb Z}=V^\star-V_{\mathbb
Z}^\star\le0.005$. Pushed \emph{adversarially} to $\sigma=0.97\LamE$ (the fiber ceiling) it is
\emph{not} always integer-tight at tiny $N$: $N{=}6$ gives $13/50$ boundary false positives
(Wilson $[16,40]\%$), closing by $N{=}8$ ($0/50$) as granularity grows.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Selection among productive optima ($30$ paired seeds)}
Single-criterion baselines (scalar-capacity, wrench-only, connectivity-only) each fail one
test by construction (certified $0\%$). Among selectors on the same productively optimal face,
the hard connectivity-constrained optimum ($\max V$ s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) reaches
$V^{\star}$ but may return \emph{any} such point and merely \emph{satisfies} the requirement
(margin $+0.15$); centralized multistart (the primal--dual solver from $4$ warm starts, best
feasible potential) gives $+0.31$. WISE is not a competitor but their lexicographic
\emph{tie-break}: it selects the maximum-margin point ($+2.55$; all $100\%$ certified, Wilson
$95\%$ CI $[89,100]$). Other fiber selectors fall short---random-fiber averages margin
$+0.10$ (sometimes infeasible) and least-role-change $+0.38$ vs WISE's $+2.53$. On scaling
(CVXPY/CLARABEL, single-threaded), Stage-1 is $\sim\!10$\,ms while the Stage-2 SDP grows with
the $(N{-}1)$-LMI ($29$\,ms/$0.26$/$2.9$/$98$\,s at $N=12/24/48/96$): fit for offline
reassignment, not yet online replanning.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Price of self-sustainability (Thm.~\ref{thm:price}, Fig.~\ref{fig:price})}
Sweeping $\sigma$ traces $\Price(\sigma)$ over $40$ seeds: zero up to $\LamE$ (free), positive
on $(\LamE,\LamX]$ (costly), infeasible beyond (impossible), matching \eqref{eq:trichotomy} on
every seed ($\LamE<\LamX$, median costly width $0.64$). The dual is a genuine price: in
unnormalized $\sigma$, $\pi^{\star}=\operatorname{tr}Y^{\star}$ matches the finite difference
$\Delta\Price/\Delta\sigma$ over $84$ interior points ($r=0.996$, median error $0.6\%$),
confirming $\pi^{\star}\in\partial\Price$ ($Z^{\star}=Y^{\star}/\pi^{\star}$;
Fig.~\ref{fig:price} normalization is display-only).
