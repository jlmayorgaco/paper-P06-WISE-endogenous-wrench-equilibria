\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region
world: a rigid load demands a planar wrench, short links connect robots only within a
cluster, and second-order unicycles apply a heading-aligned elliptical force, so the gap
is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The primary metric is the \emph{certified
service rate}: served only when wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every
instance is seeded, reproduces exactly, and is backed by a numerical certificate.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Fiber neutrality, certified (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}a)}
At an interior productive optimum $\mathcal N_p=\ker A\cap\ker B$ has dimension
$72-13=59$; moving along it holds $V$ and $Bz$ at machine precision (residuals $<10^{-8}$)
while $\lamtwo$ crosses $\sigmastar$, so the connectivity-feasible subset lies strictly
inside the optimal fiber.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Comparative-advantage exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}b,c)}
A matched long/short pair of equal lifting capacity swaps roles: in the productively
optimal but information-infeasible composition the long-range robot lifts and the short
one relays (its short range bridges nothing, $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\approx0$); WISE has the short
robot lift and the long robot relay, bridging the clusters ($\lamtwo(\Lgeo)=0.83$). Both
use the same robots and active-role count and share $Bz=y^{\star}$, $V^{\star}$, and zero
wrench residual; only connectivity differs.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Lexicographic vs.\ scalarized selection}
On free-regime seeds ($\LamE\ge\sigmastar$), the scalarised $V_\varepsilon=V+\varepsilon
\lamtwo$ trades productive value for connectivity: as $\varepsilon$ grows from $10^{-3}$ to
$1$ the productive loss rises from $0$ to $0.049$ and the aggregate drift from $0$ to
$0.307$, both under the Tikhonov envelopes, whereas the lexicographic selector holds both
at zero.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Integer recovery (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer})}
Over $30$ seeds a single randomized-rounding draw re-certifies $66\%$ of the fluid optima,
best-of-$30$ all $30$ (argmax $67\%$), with a relative welfare gap
$V_{\mathrm{relax}}-V_{\mathrm{int}}$ of $+1.8\%$ (mean, $+4.5\%$ worst). Robots are integer
throughout; the SDP certifies the relaxation, integers are re-certified.

\paragraph*{E5 --- Method comparison (Table~\ref{tab:ablation})}
At a demanding operating point ($\nu=0.4$, $\tau_d=6.5$), single-requirement and random
$\mathcal X$-feasible assignments almost never clear both tests, while the decentralized
flow (WISE-PD, $50\%$) and a centralized multistart heuristic ($67\%$) fall short of the
exact relaxed certificate. Over the $(\nu,\tau_d)$ sweep every attained solution lay inside
the SDP-feasible region (flow success $\Rightarrow$ WISE existence); certified-but-unattained
cells quantify the attainment gap.

\paragraph*{E6 --- Closed-loop transport (Fig.~\ref{fig:transport})}
Under the WISE composition the load tracks its reference, the normalized wrench residual
stays near zero, and $\lamtwo(\Lgeo^{\pi}(q(t)))$ holds above $\sigmadyn=0.2$ (min $0.58$),
with the distributed estimate $\widehat\lamtwo$ tracking within $0.011$---the margin of
Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge} in action.
