\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region
world: a rigid load demands a planar wrench, short links connect robots only within a
cluster, and the gap is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The lifters are
second-order unicycles and the load a planar rigid body,
\begin{equation}
\dot p_i=v_i[\cos\theta_i,\sin\theta_i]^{\!\top},\;\;
m_i\dot v_i=u_{v,i}-d_iv_i,\;\;J_i\dot\omega_i=u_{\omega,i}-d_{\omega i}\omega_i,
\label{eq:unicycle}
\end{equation}
with load wrench $\sum_{k,h}A_{kh}f_{i}$ from the admissible contact forces $f_i\in
U_\tau(\theta_i)$. The primary metric is the \emph{certified service rate}: served only
when wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and
reproduces exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Fiber neutrality, certified (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}a)}
At an interior productive optimum $\mathcal N_p=\ker A\cap\ker B$ has dimension $72-13=59$;
moving along it holds $V$ and $Bz$ at machine precision (residuals $<10^{-8}$) while
$\lamtwo$ crosses $\sigmastar$---the connectivity-feasible subset lies strictly inside the
optimal fiber.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Comparative-advantage exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}b,c)}
A matched long/short pair of equal lifting capacity swaps roles: in the productively
optimal but information-infeasible composition the long-range robot lifts and the short
one relays (its short range bridges nothing, $\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\approx0$); WISE has the short
robot lift and the long robot relay, bridging the clusters ($\lamtwo(\Lgeo)=0.78$). Both
use the same robots and active-role count and share $Bz=y^{\star}$, $V^{\star}$, and zero
wrench residual; only connectivity differs.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Lexicographic vs.\ scalarized selection}
On free-regime seeds the scalarised $V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$ trades productive value for
connectivity (loss $0\!\to\!0.049$, drift $0\!\to\!0.307$ as $\varepsilon:10^{-3}\!\to\!1$,
under the Tikhonov envelopes), whereas the lexicographic selector holds both at zero.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Integer recovery (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer})}
Over $30$ seeds a single randomized-rounding draw re-certifies $66\%$ of the fluid optima,
best-of-$30$ all $30$ (argmax $67\%$), with welfare gap
$V_{\mathrm{relax}}-V_{\mathrm{int}}=+1.8\%$ (mean); all robots are integer.

\paragraph*{E5 --- Method comparison (Table~\ref{tab:ablation})}
Single-criterion baselines rarely clear both tests. The hard connectivity-constrained
optimum ($\max V$ s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) reaches $V^{\star}$ but only \emph{satisfies}
the requirement (margin $+0.15$); WISE maximizes it among productive optima ($+0.99$)---the
value of the lexicographic order. The flow (WISE-PD, $50\%$) and multistart ($67\%$) attain
less than the certificate; both SDPs solve in $30$--$60$\,ms at $N=12$.

\paragraph*{E6 --- Self-defeat vs.\ self-sustenance in transport (Fig.~\ref{fig:transport})}
During transport WISE holds $\lamtwo(\Lgeo^{\pi})$ above $\sigmastar$ (min $0.58$), so the
optimizer--estimator error \emph{decays} and the load is delivered; withdrawing the relay
drops $\lamtwo$ below $\sigmadyn=\vt_1\vt_2/(c\,\mF)=0.2$ and the \emph{same} error
\emph{diverges}---the decision defeats its own computation
(Proposition~\ref{thm:stability}). The estimate $\widehat\lamtwo$ tracks within $0.011$.
