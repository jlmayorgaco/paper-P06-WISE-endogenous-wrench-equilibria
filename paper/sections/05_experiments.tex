\section{Simulation Study}
\label{sec:exp}

We validate the main results on heterogeneous teams ($N=12$ unless noted) in a two-region
world: a rigid load demands a planar wrench, short links connect robots only within a
cluster, and the gap is bridged only when a long-range type relays. The heterogeneity is
\emph{conflicting}: the long-range type has both higher lifting capacity ($c_\ell>c_s$) and
stronger relay gain ($\gamma_\ell>\gamma_s$), so it is the more valuable resource for both
roles and WISE must reorganize a whole composition to free it for the relay it alone bridges.
Integer assignments are embedded in a geometric simulator solely to evaluate the induced
communication graph $\Lgeo(q)$; closed-loop transport dynamics are outside the present
validation. The primary metric is the \emph{certified service rate}: served only when
wrench-feasible \emph{and} $\lamtwo-\sigmastar\ge0$. Every instance is seeded and reproduces
exactly.

\paragraph*{E1 --- Fiber neutrality and role exchange (Fig.~\ref{fig:central})}
At an interior optimum $\dim\mathcal N_p=72-13=59$; along it $V,Bz$ hold to $<10^{-8}$ while
$\lamtwo$ crosses $\sigmastar$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}a), so the connectivity-feasible set
lies strictly inside the fiber. Concretely (Fig.~\ref{fig:central}b,c), a matched long/short
pair (capacities equalized) swaps roles at fixed $Bz=y^{\star}$, $V^{\star}$, zero wrench
residual: the productive composition (long lifts, short relays) leaves the clusters unbridged
($\lamtwo(\Lgeo)\approx0$), whereas WISE (short lifts, long relays) bridges them
($\lamtwo(\Lgeo)=0.78$). A scalarized $V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$ instead moves the aggregate,
whereas the lexicographic form holds both value and aggregate at zero loss.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Integer recovery and an exact oracle (Rem.~\ref{rem:integer}, Table~\ref{tab:oracle})}
At $N=12$ a single randomized-rounding draw re-certifies a fluid optimum with probability
$0.40$ and best-of-$30$ re-certifies $29/30$ seeds (argmax $7/30$); the \emph{rounding loss}
$(V^{\star}_{\mathrm{relax}}-V_{\mathrm{round}})/V^{\star}_{\mathrm{relax}}=1.8\%$ (mean);
optimality is not claimed at this size. For $N\le8$ we enumerate \emph{every} $|\mathcal
A|^N$ robot--action map exactly (Table~\ref{tab:oracle}): whenever a relaxed WISE exists an
integer self-sustaining assignment also exists ($0/23$ false positives observed), and the
productive integrality gap $g_{\mathbb Z}=V^\star-V_{\mathbb Z}^\star$ is $\le0.002$ (an exact
zero-cost integer WISE, $B\hat z=y^\star$, is rarer, but the productive loss is negligible).

\paragraph*{E3 --- Selection among productive optima ($30$ paired seeds)}
Single-criterion baselines (scalar-capacity, wrench-only, connectivity-only) each fail one
test by construction (certified $0\%$). Among selectors on the same productively optimal face,
the hard connectivity-constrained optimum ($\max V$ s.t.\ $\lamtwo\ge\sigmastar$) reaches
$V^{\star}$ but may return \emph{any} such point and merely \emph{satisfies} the requirement
(margin $+0.15$); centralized multistart (the primal--dual solver from $4$ warm starts, best
feasible potential) gives $+0.31$. WISE is not a competitor but their lexicographic
\emph{tie-break}: it selects the maximum-margin point ($+2.55$; all $100\%$ certified, Wilson
$95\%$ CI $[89,100]$), and the relaxed SDP solves in $30$--$60$\,ms at $N=12$.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Price of self-sustainability (Thm.~\ref{thm:price}, Fig.~\ref{fig:price})}
Sweeping $\sigma$ traces $\Price(\sigma)$ over $40$ seeds: zero up to $\LamE$ (free), positive
on $(\LamE,\LamX]$ (costly), infeasible beyond (impossible), matching \eqref{eq:trichotomy} on
every seed ($\LamE<\LamX$, median costly width $0.64$). The subgradient
$\pi^{\star}\in\partial\Price$ prices one unit of connectivity.
