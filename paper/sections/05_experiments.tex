\paragraph*{E1 --- Affine model: the WISE SDP and its certificate ($N{=}12$)}
Relay sites here are anchored and every relay-gated link uses a precomputed tube containing
all admissible receiver positions, so each $L_{ir}$ is fixed before optimization and
independent of the other robots' actions: Assumption~\ref{as:standing}(ii) holds exactly. With
$n=72$ and $\dim\Emanifold=59$ (residuals $<10^{-15}$), Table~\ref{tab:glance}A reports the
local--global equivalence, the modulus at the selector, the relaxed re-check, and the margin
$m_\lambda$ separating WISE from the hard-threshold selector \emph{inside} the model the
theorem covers.

\paragraph*{E2 --- Pair-dependent integer stress test ($N{=}6$, Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}(a))}
Here tube infima depend on \emph{both} endpoints' actions, so the induced graph $\Lpair(z)$ is
\emph{not} affine in $z$: this instance lies outside Assumption~\ref{as:standing}(ii),
Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability} and \eqref{eq:sdp}, and no claim below invokes them. Replacing one long lifter by two short ones and sending the released
long robot to a relay site holds $Bz=(3,3)=y^{\star}$ and $V=V^{\star}$ exactly, with both
nominal wrench certificates granted by \eqref{eq:tensor}, while $\lamtwo(\Lpair)$ rises
$0\to0.399$ at no loss of $V$. Exhaustive enumeration of all $|\mathcal A|^N=12^6$ maps leaves $3864$ wrench-feasible and
$1032$ on $\Emanifold$, proving the \emph{integer} capacity
$\Lambda^{\mathbb Z}_{\Emanifold,\mathrm{pair}}
=\max_{\Emanifold\cap\{0,1\}^n}\lamtwo(\Lpair)=0.3989$, which does not bound the relaxed affine
$\LamE$. Imposing the action-independent affine surrogate instead deletes the two links that
distinguish relay choices, their worst-case separation $5.20$ exceeding the $5.0$ range; under
that conservative surrogate every clearing assignment has the same $\lamtwo$, so the affine
model cannot separate HARD from pair-WISE \emph{in this geometry}, which is why we report it
only as an exact integer stress test.

\paragraph*{E3 --- Planar closed-loop transport (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}(b),(c))}
\emph{Setup.} Two rigid loads with $M_k\dot\nu_k+D_k\nu_k=\sum_{i\in\mathcal C_k}A_{kh(i)}f_i
+d_k(t)$ follow curved paths and resynchronize after a prescribed drag disturbance. At each
$20$\,Hz step a trajectory-PD demand is met by a minimum-effort allocator restricted to the
\emph{same} $U_{\tau(i)}^{\mathrm{in}}$ the certificate uses, while
$\Lgeo(q(t))$, rebuilt from the simulated poses, drives \eqref{eq:interconnection}, whose
state sets each coalition's progress rate $\dot s_k=v_0h_k(t)\,\mathrm{mean}_{i\in\mathcal C_k}
\mathrm{sat}_{[0,2]}(u_0-k_a[Qa]_i)$, $v_0=1/48$\,s$^{-1}$, $u_0=k_a=1$, $h_k$ a first-order
governor. Both selectors are exact optima over the same enumerated integer fiber
$\Emanifold_{\mathbb Z}$, declared before the sweep:
$z_{\mathrm{HARD}}\in\arg\min\{c_{\mathrm{role}}(z,z^{\mathrm{prod}}):z\in\Emanifold_{\mathbb Z},
\lamtwo(\Lpair(z))\ge\sigmastar\}$ and
$z_{\text{pair-WISE}}\in\arg\max_{\Emanifold_{\mathbb Z}}\lamtwo(\Lpair(z))$, with
$c_{\mathrm{role}}$ counting role changes from $z^{\mathrm{prod}}$ and ties broken by
deployment cost then action index.

\emph{Certified.} All three hold $V=V^{\star}$ and both nominal wrench certificates, differing
only in reserve. Cor.~\ref{cor:budget} applies although $\Lpair$ is not affine in $z$, needing
only $\Lpair^{\rho}=(1{-}\rho)\Lpair+\rho L_0$ affine in $\rho$ at a \emph{fixed} assignment;
with $\lamtwo(L_0)=0$ it gives $10.8\%$ against $24.8\%$ (Table~\ref{tab:glance}).

\emph{Observed.} On the predeclared $0.01$ grid of $\Lpair^{\rho}$, everything else fixed, the
curves stay above $\sigmastar$ beyond those bounds, first falling below at $\rho=0.12$ and
$0.28$ (Table~\ref{tab:glance}). Feasibility is re-checked at \emph{every} control step: the
demand is met to $6\cdot10^{-8}$ outside the disturbance window, and inside it the team
saturates (peak residual $0.82$\,N) while the governor slows the load. Geometric transfer holds
\emph{conditionally on the tubes}: over $9.6\cdot10^{5}$ samples
$\lamtwo(\Lgeo)-\lamtwo(\Lpair(\hat z))$ has median $+0.065$ and minimum $+0.031$ with zero
tube exits, though the controller does not enforce invariance. The information layer decays at
observed fitted rates $0.156$ (WISE) and $0.108$ (HARD), both above $\alpha(\sigmastar)=0.0397$
(Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}), whereas PROD \emph{grows} by $5.3\cdot10^{5}$. Over $30$ paired worlds (mass $\pm10\%$, damping $\pm15\%$, initial pose, disturbance
amplitude and onset; the seed is the unit, all parametric variations of the \emph{same}
structural scenario) HARD and WISE succeed $30/30$ against $0/30$ for PROD and a random fiber
point. WISE's paired median peak synchronization error is a reduction of $0.733$
$[0.713,0.749]$ against PROD but $+1.7\cdot10^{-4}$ $[-2.5,5.5]\cdot10^{-4}$ against HARD:
indistinguishable at mission level, the gain being the larger certified reserve and the faster
information-layer decay.

\paragraph*{E4 --- Integer recovery, oracle, regimes and price ($N{=}12$ unless stated)}
On the tested $N\le8$ instances an exact oracle enumerates \emph{every} $|\mathcal A|^N$ map,
the maximum integer self-sustaining gap is below $0.06\%$ of $V^{\star}$ and no false positive
occurs at the design requirement ($0/23$); at $\sigma=0.97\LamE$ relaxed feasibility fails to
imply integer feasibility in $13/50$ tested $N{=}6$ instances and in $0/50$ at $N{=}7,8$.
Separately, at $N=12$ structured rounding is jointly feasible in $895/900$ draws after repair
with median loss $4.2\%$, a per-instance rate of $99.4\%$ under a cluster bootstrap treating
the instance, not the draw, as the unit. Sweeping the long-range-type fraction $\nu$ against the wrench-demand tightness $\tau_d$
($6\times6$ cells, $4$ seeds) activates no wrench facet and leaves $\LamE$ invariant along
$\tau_d$; at $\tau_d=8$ the first facet binds and $\Price$ traces the predicted trichotomy,
its dual matching the finite difference ($r=0.996$). A scalarized $V+\varepsilon\lamtwo$
drifts the aggregate by up to $0.284$ over $\varepsilon\in[10^{-3},1]$.
