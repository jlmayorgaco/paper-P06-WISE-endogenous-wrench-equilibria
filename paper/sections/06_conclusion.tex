\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We identified composition degeneracy as a resource rather than a nuisance in
heterogeneous cooperative transport. When the productive optimum fixes the served
aggregate but not its composition by type (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a secondary
spectral program selects, among equally productive assignments, one that preserves the
communication substrate the distributed estimator needs. WISE existence and selection
reduce to a semidefinite program (Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}), and a Schur-complement
condition ties the connectivity requirement to exponential stability of the
optimizer--estimator subsystem (Theorem~\ref{thm:stability}); simulations with
heterogeneous second-order unicycles reproduce the feasibility boundary, separate wrench
from communication infeasibility, and show relay allocation tracking each type's spectral
benefit against its productive opportunity cost.

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the relaxed candidate-site model: $\Lap(z)$ is an affine
surrogate bounding the geometric graph $\Lgeo(q)$ through Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integer
assignments are re-certified after rounding (Remark~\ref{rem:integer}); nonholonomic
wrench realizability is modelled by the heading-aligned force set, not proved; and
validation is in simulation. A realizability theorem for the nonholonomic contact and
hardware validation remain open.
