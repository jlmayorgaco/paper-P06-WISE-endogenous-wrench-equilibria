\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource: when the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate
but not its composition (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a tangent-cone criterion certifies
zero-cost connectivity gains and, when it vanishes, global connectivity optimality on the
whole fiber (Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability}); a lexicographic SDP selects the most-connected
optimum (Thm.~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the requirement
(Cor.~\ref{thm:price}), and clearing $\sigmadyn$ buys certified information-layer stability
(Prop.~\ref{thm:stability}) at $\Delta V=0$.

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the affine relaxation ($\Lgeo\succeq\Lap$ by lower-bound
Laplacians, Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integers re-certified, Prop.~\ref{prop:integer}); actuator-level
realization and closed-loop transport are outside this study. A distributed realization with a
convergence guarantee, a scalable oracle beyond $N=8$, and hardware validation remain open.
