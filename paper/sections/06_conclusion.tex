\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource in heterogeneous cooperative transport: when the
productive optimum fixes the served aggregate but not its composition
(Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a tangent-cone criterion certifies zero-cost connectivity
gains (Prop.~\ref{prop:freeconn}), a lexicographic semidefinite program selects the
most-connected optimum (Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the
requirement (Theorem~\ref{thm:price}), and explicit margins transfer the certificate to an
integer team (Prop.~\ref{prop:integer}). A small-gain argument only \emph{motivates} the
threshold (Prop.~\ref{thm:stability}).

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the affine communication-backbone relaxation ($\Lap(z)$ is within a
measured perturbation $\varepsilon_L$ of $\Lgeo(q)$, Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integers are
re-certified after rounding, Prop.~\ref{prop:integer}); nonholonomic realizability is
modelled, not proved. A distributed online realization with a convergence guarantee, an
a-priori mismatch bound, an exact integer oracle, and hardware validation remain open.
