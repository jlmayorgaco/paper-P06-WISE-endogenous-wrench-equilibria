\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource. When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate
but not its composition (Thm.~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a single scalar decides whether free
connectivity remains and, when it vanishes, certifies global connectivity optimality over the
whole fiber (Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability}); a lexicographic SDP selects that optimum
(Thm.~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the requirement
(Cor.~\ref{thm:price}), and clearing $\sigmadyn$ buys certified information-layer stability at
$\Delta V=0$---with the same certified lower rate bound while the graph moves
(Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}). A planar closed-loop transport simulation turns that into a mission
outcome: the re-certified integer WISE team keeps the served aggregate, the certified wrenches
and the communication lower bound, and resynchronizes two loads that the productively identical
disconnected assignment cannot. Guarantees hold for the affine
relaxation and transfer to the geometric graph only at a re-certified integer assignment;
actuator-level realization, unilateral contact, distributed realization and hardware validation
remain open.
