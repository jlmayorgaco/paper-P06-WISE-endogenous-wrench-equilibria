\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We identified composition degeneracy as a resource in heterogeneous cooperative transport.
When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate but not its composition by type
(Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a secondary spectral program selects, among equally
productive assignments, one that preserves the communication substrate the estimator
needs; relaxed WISE existence and selection reduce to a semidefinite program
(Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}), and a small-gain condition ties the requirement to
optimizer--estimator stability (Proposition~\ref{thm:stability}).

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the relaxed candidate-site model: $\Lap(z)$ is an affine surrogate
bounding $\Lgeo(q)$ through Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integer assignments are re-certified
after rounding (Remark~\ref{rem:integer}); nonholonomic realizability is modelled, not
proved; and validation is in simulation. A realizability theorem for the nonholonomic
contact and hardware validation remain open.
