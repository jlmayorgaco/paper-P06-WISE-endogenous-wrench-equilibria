\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource. When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate
but not its composition (Thm.~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), $\Gamma_{\Emanifold}$ decides whether free
connectivity remains and, when it vanishes, certifies global \emph{connectivity} optimality
over the whole fiber (Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability}); a lexicographic SDP selects that point
(Thm.~\ref{thm:existence}), $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the requirement (Sec.~\ref{sec:price}), and
clearing $\sigmadyn$ certifies information-layer stability at $\Delta V=0$ at a rate surviving
a moving graph (Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}). That reserve is an operational budget
(Cor.~\ref{cor:budget}): on the $N{=}6$ stress test pair-WISE tolerates at least $24.8\%$ relay
attenuation against $10.8\%$, at identical productive value. The closed loop exercises exactly
the fixed-assignment results (Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}, Cors.~\ref{cor:tv},~\ref{cor:budget}),
while Thms.~\ref{thm:degeneracy}--\ref{thm:existence} are validated on the affine family;
closing that loop on an $N{=}12$ instance, actuator-level realization, unilateral contact and
hardware remain open.
