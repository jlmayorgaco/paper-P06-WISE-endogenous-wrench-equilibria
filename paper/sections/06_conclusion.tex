\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource. When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate but
not its composition (Thm.~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), one scalar decides whether free connectivity
remains and, when it vanishes, certifies global connectivity optimality over the whole fiber
(Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability}); a lexicographic SDP selects that optimum
(Thm.~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the requirement
(Cor.~\ref{thm:price}), and clearing $\sigmadyn$ buys certified information-layer stability at
$\Delta V=0$---at a rate bound that survives a moving graph (Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}). That reserve
is an operational budget (Cor.~\ref{cor:budget}): WISE is certified to tolerate at least $24.8\%$ relay
attenuation against a hard-threshold selector's $10.8\%$, at identical productive value, while a planar
closed-loop study shows the productively identical \emph{disconnected} assignment failing
outright. Guarantees hold for the affine relaxation
and transfer to the geometric graph only at a re-certified integer assignment; actuator-level
realization, unilateral contact, distribution and hardware validation remain open.
