\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource. When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate
but not its composition (Thm.~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), one scalar decides whether free
connectivity remains and, when it vanishes, certifies global connectivity optimality over the
whole fiber (Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability}); a lexicographic SDP selects that optimum
(Thm.~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the requirement
(Cor.~\ref{thm:price}), and clearing $\sigmadyn$ certifies information-layer stability at
$\Delta V=0$, at a rate bound that survives a moving graph (Cor.~\ref{cor:tv}). That reserve is
an operational budget (Cor.~\ref{cor:budget}): on the $N{=}6$ stress test WISE is certified to
tolerate at least $24.8\%$ relay attenuation against a hard-threshold selector's $10.8\%$ at
identical productive value. Guarantees hold for the affine relaxation and transfer to the
geometric graph only at a re-certified integer assignment; actuator-level realization,
unilateral contact, distributed execution and hardware validation remain open.
