\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We identified composition degeneracy as a resource in heterogeneous cooperative
transport. When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate but not its composition
by type (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a secondary spectral program selects, among
equally productive assignments, one that preserves the communication substrate the
distributed estimator needs. WISE existence and selection reduce to a semidefinite program
(Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}), and a Schur-complement condition ties the connectivity
requirement to exponential stability of the optimizer--estimator subsystem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:stability}); simulations certify the payoff-flat fiber, the safe/unsafe
split on it, and the lexicographic advantage over scalarization.

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the relaxed candidate-site model: $\Lap(z)$ is an affine
surrogate bounding the geometric graph $\Lgeo(q)$ through Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integer
assignments are re-certified after rounding (Remark~\ref{rem:integer}); nonholonomic
wrench realizability is modelled by the heading-aligned force set, not proved; and
validation is in simulation. A realizability theorem for the nonholonomic contact and
hardware validation remain open.
