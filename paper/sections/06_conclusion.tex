\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource. When the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate
but not its composition (Thm.~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a single scalar decides whether free
connectivity remains and, when it vanishes, certifies global connectivity optimality over the
whole fiber (Thm.~\ref{thm:networkability}); a lexicographic SDP selects that optimum
(Thm.~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the requirement
(Cor.~\ref{thm:price}), and clearing $\sigmadyn$ buys certified information-layer stability at
$\Delta V=0$. The guarantees hold for the affine relaxation, transferring to the geometric
graph only at a re-certified integer assignment; actuator realization, closed-loop transport,
a distributed realization, and hardware validation remain open.
