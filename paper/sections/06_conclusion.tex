\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We identified composition degeneracy as a resource rather than a nuisance in
heterogeneous cooperative transport. When the productive optimum fixes the served
aggregate but not its composition by type (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a secondary
spectral program can select, among equally productive assignments, one that preserves
the communication substrate the distributed estimator needs.

WISE existence and selection reduce to a semidefinite program
(Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}), and a Schur-complement condition ties its connectivity
requirement to exponential stability of the served-aggregate/estimator subsystem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:stability}). Simulations with heterogeneous second-order unicycles
reproduce the predicted feasibility boundary, separate wrench infeasibility from
communication infeasibility, and show relay allocation tracking each type's spectral
benefit against its productive opportunity cost.

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the relaxed candidate-site model: the induced Laplacian
$\Lap(z)$ is an affine surrogate for the nonconvex geometric graph $\Lgeo(q)$, which it
bounds only through Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integer assignments are re-certified after
rounding (Remark~\ref{rem:integer}); nonholonomic wrench realizability is modelled by
the heading-aligned force set, not proved; and validation is in simulation. A
realizability theorem for the nonholonomic contact, an analysis of the geometric graph
$\Lgeo(q)$, and hardware validation remain open.
