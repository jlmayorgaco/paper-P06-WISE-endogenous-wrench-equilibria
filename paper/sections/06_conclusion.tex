\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Composition degeneracy is a resource: when the productive optimum fixes the served aggregate
but not its composition (Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneracy}), a tangent-cone criterion certifies
zero-cost connectivity gains (Prop.~\ref{prop:freeconn}), a lexicographic SDP selects the
most-connected optimum (Theorem~\ref{thm:existence}), the capacities $\LamE\le\LamX$ price the
requirement (Theorem~\ref{thm:price}), and a margin test preserves feasibility after integer
recovery (Prop.~\ref{prop:integer}).

\paragraph*{Limitations and future work}
The guarantees hold for the affine communication-backbone relaxation ($\Lap(z)$ is within a
measured perturbation $\varepsilon_L$ of $\Lgeo(q)$, Lemma~\ref{lem:bridge}; integers are
re-certified after rounding, Prop.~\ref{prop:integer}); actuator-level realization and
closed-loop transport are outside this study. A distributed online realization with a
convergence guarantee, a scalable exact integer oracle beyond $N=8$, and hardware validation
remain open.
